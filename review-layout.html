Changed 18 September

The soft photo edges are gone

Blending the photo edges into the page looked good where a photo had space around its subject, but where somebody stood near the edge it dissolved them. A scan of every photo in the book found about a hundred at risk, and a dozen pages had already been reported. Photos now print with a clean edge. The gold frames stay off, which was the other half of the request.
