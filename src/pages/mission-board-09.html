2026 — A Jubilee Year of Service

In 2026, the Mission Board carried its work forward through community partnerships, supporting local students and families and bringing a little joy to children facing cancer.

The parish collected canned goods for Mother of Mercy House and prepared Easter baskets for children through the Emmanuel Cancer Foundation. The Back-to-School drive supported Loesche Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls and the Bensalem Township School District. A special highlight came in October, when the Bensalem Township School District recognized SGMOC for its clothing donation to local students and families.

Plans for the remainder of the year include serving Thanksgiving dinner to the Snyder-Girotti school community, packing Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes and singing Christmas carols with nursing home residents.

Feature: Rise Against Hunger

Rise Against Hunger is an international hunger relief organization that coordinates the packaging and distribution of food and other life-changing aid to people in developing nations. Our parish has joined its mission twice in this record, and each time with the same invitation printed on our flyer: #ItStartsWithAMeal, and it starts with you.

November 16, 2019: Pack 10,152 Meals

The Fish & Loaves team, led by Neethu Varghese, hosted a meal-packing event at SGMOC from 8 AM to 12:30 PM, calling for 100 volunteers and setting a goal of packing 10,152 meals to “make a difference in a family’s life.”
