SGMOC Mission Board

To our ministry leaders, the Mission Board’s Ministry Representatives and the many project leads and teams who planned, phoned, packed, and drove: Joe John, Mathew M. Kurian, Dr. Jesty Moses, Alex Thankachan, and Thomas Joseph, who served as Managing Committee and Board representatives and authored annual reports with Vincy Wilson; the leaders of the Sunday School Mission Team since 2018, including Dr. Silvi Chacko, Reny Biju, Vincy Wilson, and Caroline Varughese; and the leaders of MMVS, GSM, Men’s Forum, G.R.O.W., S.T.G., Youth League, and the Acolytes: thank you for turning good intentions into good works.

The Mission Board gave the generosity of these ministries a coordinated form. In 2024, the Managing Committee and Board approved 16 charity applications totaling $12,000 in donations, while MMVS, GSM, Sunday School, and Youth League conducted 17 charity drives and events totaling approximately $15,000. These are separate measures of support. In 2025, parish charitable contributions were approximately $25,000. Behind every figure are people who donated, organized, delivered, and prayed—and those whose lives were touched by kindness.

A Jubilee Year of Recognition

As we give thanks for the work of our children, families, and volunteers, we also remember the recognition received from the wider Bensalem community. In October 2026, the Bensalem Township School District publicly recognized SGMOC for its donation of clothing to local students and families. The certificate and photograph record a moment of gratitude for service that extends beyond the parish.

To our children, who have carried shoeboxes, prepared care packages, made blankets, sung carols, written letters, and served Thanksgiving meals: you remind us that even small hands can make a lasting impact. To our families, who have given generously, encouraged their children to serve, and delivered supplies to schools: the heart of this mission is the family.

And to our volunteers, all members of SGMOC whose time, effort, and faithful stewardship have been the driving force behind the Mission Board: thank you. Your work has touched hundreds of lives locally and around the world, and thousands through our partners. We pray that the next generation will take this same spirit of joyful service into the future.

A Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, You multiplied the loaves and fed the multitude. Receive the small gifts we have offered in Your name, bless the hands that gave them, and keep us ever faithful to proclaim, by our deeds, a Jubilee of mercy for all. Amen.
