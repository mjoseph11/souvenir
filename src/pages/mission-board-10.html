September 2023: Sponsor, Paint, Pack

In 2023, the parish once again partnered with Rise Against Hunger, bringing the community together through fundraising, creative activities, donations and hands-on service to support the goal of providing 20,000 meals. Families participated in a special painting event featuring mugs and bowls, raising $1,075 for the meal-packing initiative. The effort culminated on September 30, when volunteers packed 20,088 meals, exceeding the 20,000-meal goal and demonstrating the parish’s continued commitment to fighting hunger through generosity, fellowship and community service.

Parish volunteers packing meals at the Rise Against Hunger event, September 2023.

The icon of the feeding of the multitude that graces our Mission Board reports reminds us that Christ takes what we offer, however small, and multiplies it. A 39-cent meal is a small offering. Ten thousand of them are a feast.

Feature: Back to School, Year After Year

Education is a gift that outlasts a season, and our Back to School drive has become one of the most beloved works of the parish. Its story runs through every year of this record.

2018: The Backpack Project reached children in Kerala and the USA, with 288 backpacks distributed by December.

2019: Uniforms and stationery for Loesche Elementary School, folders for 128 students at the Philadelphia girls’ high school, and supplies for a school nurse’s office.

2021: The drive supported Loesche Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls, Tilden Middle School and Snyder-Girotti Elementary School with uniforms, stationery and other essentials, including 32 backpacks for Snyder-Girotti and a $500 contribution for Tilden.

2022: In September, the Sunday School donated school supplies to four Philadelphia-area schools.

2023: The SGMOC Charity Fund, together with parish contributions, supported uniforms for 50 students at William Loesche Elementary School, school supplies for the Philadelphia High School for Girls, hygiene products for Joseph Greenberg Elementary School, and uniforms, school supplies and educational aids for students with special needs at Henry Lawton Elementary School, along with annual education sponsorship for two students through Deepalaya in India.
