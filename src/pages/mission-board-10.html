SGMOC Mission Board

Caring for Families and Little Ones

Project Linus. In January and February 2024, led by Caroline Varughese with Vincy Wilson, Shainey Jacob, Sharon Alex and Anna Philip, the SGMOC family crafted 27 blankets to comfort children in need through this national nonprofit that provides children with a security blanket.

Diaper Drive. In April 2024, led by Siji Chacko with Jeni Chacko, Caroline Varughese and Vincy Wilson, we collaborated with Family Service (The Audrey J. Tucker Resource Center) and YWCA Bucks County and distributed 7,820 diapers and 16,960 wipes for adults and children. In 2025 more than 60 boxes of diapers and wipes were collected and donated to the Family Service Association of Bucks County, easing the financial burden on families and promoting “the well-being and dignity of those served.”

Bibs-n-Bottles Drive. In December 2024, in collaboration with the Bucks County Family Services Association, Shyno Thomas and Vincy Wilson led a drive for children under the age of five. Our parishioners donated 4,032 wipes, 822 diapers, 10 diaper creams, 40 pacifiers, 50 bottles and 42 blankets.

Standing with the Homeless and Hungry

Feed Our Community. In March 2024, 40 volunteers from SGMOC and St. Luke came together to prepare and distribute 187 meal packets through Mother of Mercy House in Kensington, Philadelphia. Each packet included a sandwich, cookies, fruit and a bottle of water, extending our care to neighbors experiencing homelessness.
