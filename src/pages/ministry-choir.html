Our Choir

Serving God through music and worship

“Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth.”

— Psalm 96:1

The Church Choir Ministry is a dedicated group of Orthodox believers who use their God-given musical talents to glorify God, lead the congregation, and enhance the worship experience. As an essential part of our Orthodox tradition, the choir helps create an atmosphere that draws the congregation closer to God’s presence during the Divine Liturgy.

Orthodox tones and musical settings are among the most beautiful in the world. As choir members, we honor God with humility and dedication, creating an uplifting atmosphere through well-sung hymns during Holy Qurbana that encourages worshippers to deeply immerse themselves in the liturgy.

Our mission is to glorify God with our voices. Beyond the beauty of the music, we aim to convey the deeper meaning of what is sung to lead the congregation in meaningful worship. We invite everyone to join us in enriching the spiritual life of our SGMOC community.
