Beginning in 2018, SGMOC also hosted the Unity Prayer Fellowship as part of its involvement with Building a Better Bensalem Together (B3T), bringing together members of the wider community in fellowship and prayer. When the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted normal life in 2020, SGMOC was well prepared to meet the unprecedented challenge. The parish had already established livestreaming services to enable those unable to attend church due to age, illness, or other circumstances to participate in worship from their homes. This existing capability proved invaluable during the pandemic. While many places of worship were forced to suspend or reduce their services, SGMOC continued its regular schedule of worship without interruption, reaching a record number of faithful through online participation. During this difficult period, the parish also continued to support its members and the surrounding community, demonstrating its enduring commitment to faith, fellowship, and service.

In 2023, SGMOC mourned the passing of its founding Vicar, Late Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos. From the establishment of the parish in 1976 through decades of challenge, growth, and transformation, Mathai Achen had played a defining role in the journey of SGMOC. His passing marked the loss of a beloved spiritual father whose ministry and legacy remain deeply woven into the history of the parish.

Golden Jubilee and a New Chapter – 2026

The year 2026 became one of the most blessed and memorable years in the history of SGMOC as we celebrated our Golden Jubilee, marking fifty years since the establishment of our parish in 1976. Our Jubilee celebration brought together generations of our parish family along with distinguished guests, including Congressman Brian Fitzpatrick, State Senator Frank Farry, State Representative Kathleen Tomlinson, Bensalem Mayor Joseph DiGirolamo, and other community leaders. During the public meeting, we honored many of our pioneers, senior members, past and present office bearers, and clergy whose faith, sacrifice, and leadership helped shape our parish. The celebration continued with a memorable cultural program presented by our own members and the inauguration of new initiatives, including the Memorial Wall and Vision 2030 and Beyond, looking toward the future of SGMOC.
