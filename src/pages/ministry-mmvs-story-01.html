The Women Who Built Our Church: The Story of Morth Mariam Vanitha Samajam (MMVS)

The Women's Ministry of SGMOC

Long before it carried a name, the spirit of Morth Mariam Vanitha Samajam (MMVS) was already alive within St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church. From the very founding of the parish in 1976, the women of SGMOC gathered in one another's homes for worship and prayer, planting the seeds of a ministry that would grow alongside the church itself.

It was in 1982, when the North East American Diocese formally established MMVS at the diocesan level, that this quiet devotion found its official structure. The diocesan initiative gave rise to the MMVS wing within SGMOC, formalizing a ministry the women of the parish had already been living out for years.

The Initial Years

As a small women's ministry unit in its earliest days, MMVS was led by Very Rev. K. Mathai as President, supported by Rev. Fr. Paul Varki and Dn. Markose Mani, the late Mrs. Saramma Mathai, as its first Secretary, alongside Mrs. Aleyamma Mani, as its first Treasurer. When the official MMVS ministry was established at the NE American Diocese level in 1982, the SGMOC unit elected Mrs. Aleyamma Mani as Secretary and Mrs. Rachel Parel as Treasurer, marking the ministry's transition into its formal structure.

In its earliest years, MMVS took shape through simple, faithful routines that grounded the ministry in prayer and service. Monthly prayer meetings became a cornerstone of parish life, with members encouraged to share a message drawn from the Scripture portion they had read that morning — turning each gathering into a time of both personal reflection and communal prayer.

MMVS also took on the joyful responsibility of serving food after Holy Qurbana every Sunday. Nothing was ever ordered from outside; the food was cooked at home and brought to the church each Sunday morning, a personal offering of love and labor for the parish family.
