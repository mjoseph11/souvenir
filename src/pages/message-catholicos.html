His Holiness Baselios Marthoma Mathews III

Catholicos of the East & Malankara Metropolitan
Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church

Beloved in the Lord, I extend my Catholicate blessings and greetings to the clergy, faithful, and well-wishers of St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem, Pennsylvania, as you joyfully celebrate the Golden Jubilee of your holy parish.

Fifty years is a testament to the grace of God and the steadfast faith of this community. From its humble beginnings to its present flourishing, the parish has been a beacon of the apostolic Orthodox faith in the American Diaspora. May St. Gregorios of Parumala, in whose holy name this parish is consecrated, continue to intercede for all of you.
