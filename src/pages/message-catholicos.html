Message

From the Catholicos

Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church

His Holiness Baselios Marthoma Mathews III

Catholicos of the Apostolic Throne of St. Thomas
and Malankara Metropolitan

On the Occasion of the Golden Jubilee of St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem, Pennsylvania, USA

“For the LORD is good; his steadfast love endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.”
Psalm 100:5

Dearly beloved in Christ,

The words of the Psalmist remind us that God’s goodness and faithfulness have sustained His people through every generation. As St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem, celebrates fifty years of its life and witness, we give thanks to Almighty God for His abundant grace upon this parish. What began with the prayers and dedication of a few faithful has, by God’s grace, grown into a spiritual home for generations of Orthodox Christians.

We remember with gratitude the founding members, the clergy and all those who have laboured and prayed for the growth of this parish. Their faith and commitment have enabled the generations that followed to remain steadfast in the faith of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church.

As we celebrate this Golden Jubilee, let us continue to uphold the apostolic faith, participate faithfully in the Holy Qurbana and strengthen our life of prayer and fellowship. Let us also take care to pass on to our children the faith handed down to us.

We extend our heartfelt felicitations to the Vicar, the office-bearers and all the faithful. May the intercession of St. Gregorios of Parumala and the Holy Theotokos be with you all.

May the love of God the Father, the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen.

With prayers and paternal blessings.
Baselios Marthoma Mathews III
November 9, 2026
