Our Sunday School

Before 1992|Dn. Markose Mani

SGMOC Sunday School

Before being ordained as a deacon, K.M. Mani was active in various capacities at SGMOC. One of his most cherished roles was that of Sunday School teacher; he felt that passing on the values and traditions of the Indian Orthodox Church was paramount to its continuation in the United States.

During his tenure as head of the Sunday School program at SGMOC, he was dedicated to teaching children about the Bible, the importance of the rituals in the Qurbana, as well as the stories of believers who led exemplary lives following Christ’s example.

His experiences in helping establish an Orthodox church in Rourkela, Orissa (India) shaped his views on the importance of preserving the church’s history and making sure children are aware of the sacrifices of their elders.

1992–1996|Lee K. George

My Journey in Sunday School

My journey as a Sunday School teacher spanned about a decade. It began in the early 1990s as an advisor to the youth league and a teacher for the combined high school classes. At the time, we worshiped in a rented church at 38th and Chestnut, where limited space meant Sunday School classes were held during morning prayers before Holy Qurbana.

At the end of 1992, then-vicar Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos asked me to serve as Headmaster, a role I held through 1996. I fondly remember bringing together churches across Philadelphia for a combined OVBS because the smaller churches did not have enough students to hold programs individually. This became an important part of Sunday School life in the Philadelphia area. Another meaningful change was transforming the annual caroling service from a nighttime event in which only men participated into one led by the Youth League and Sunday School, allowing everyone—boys and girls, men and women—to participate in this annual celebration of Christmas beginning in 1994. The move to the Torresdale Church significantly enhanced our Sunday School program, giving each grade its own classroom space.

I thank Almighty God for the opportunity to serve in this capacity and pray for the continued success of our Sunday School Mission.

1998–2013|Paul J. Maret

A Journey of Faith and Service

My journey as Sunday School Principal began in 1998, when our founding vicar, Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos, entrusted me with the responsibility of leading our Sunday School. Accepting this role was not easy, as our first child, Josh, was only a few months old. During those early years at our church on Torresdale Avenue in Philadelphia, we faced several challenges, including limited space and a small number of dedicated Sunday School teachers.

Despite these challenges, we remained committed to providing our children with a strong spiritual foundation. We conducted general sessions alongside individual class lessons to ensure that our students received a well-rounded Sunday School education. I am especially grateful to our Sunday School teacher at the time, Mrs. Mercy Varkey, who took the lead in conducting the general sessions and provided invaluable support throughout this period.

Our students also began building a strong record of participation and achievement through centralized examinations and talent competitions. One of the major highlights of these early years was hosting the area-level Orthodox Vacation Bible School (OVBS) at our church. This program was successfully conducted under the leadership of Mr. Korah T. Mani, who served as the Philadelphia Area Sunday School Coordinator at the time.
