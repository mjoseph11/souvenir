Our Sunday School

Building Fellowship

Sonday Funday — family fellowship.

Our theme, Faith, Fun, and Fellowship, came alive through Family Nights, apple picking, pumpkin carving, Ice Cream Socials, and especially Sonday Funday. These events strengthened friendships among children, parents, teachers, and volunteers while reinforcing that the Church is one family.

Looking Back with Gratitude

Looking back, I treasure not only the programs we introduced but the relationships that were formed. I remain deeply grateful to Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai, our leadership team, teachers, volunteers, parents, and students. My prayer is that our efforts helped create a Sunday School where every child felt welcomed and experienced Faith, Fun, and Fellowship while growing closer to Christ.
