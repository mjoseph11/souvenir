SGMOC Mission Board

Through Be the Bridge, children met virtually with parish seniors, families with newborns and those facing difficult times, sharing Scripture, prayers, songs and their God-given talents. Our Sunday School children continued writing to 15 seniors at Woodbourne Nursing Home, sharing everyday experiences, favorite books and hobbies, and asking their older friends for advice.

Through Ivins Outreach, our monthly food support reached 20 families, and we shared the joy of Christmas by providing gifts to 15 families. Our spring diaper drive, led by Siji John, provided 7,521 diapers and 11,792 wipes through the Philadelphia Diaper Drive. A $250 Target gift card also helped an elderly mother purchase adult diapers.

Children shared a virtual Christmas celebration with Woodbourne residents through songs, instrumental performances and Scripture readings. During the 2021–2022 season, the team collected and delivered new and gently used clothing for Afghan refugee families in collaboration with The Wardrobe, Cradles and Crayons, and Replay.

“In a world where you can be anything, be kind.”

— Mother Teresa, quoted in our CARES points program

Distance changed how we served, never why we served. In the hardest season many of us have known, our children discovered that kindness needs no building, only a willing heart.

2022 — Reconnecting and Reaching Out

As the pandemic eased, our children found new ways to bring people together through letters, meals, drives and songs. Some activities carried forward the relationships built during the pandemic, while others brought children, parents and teachers together to serve in person again.

Our students exchanged letters with seniors at Woodbourne Nursing Home from January through December. In February, they tucked snacks and handwritten notes into bags of socks for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter as part of the winter coat and sock drive. In April, they collected children’s and adult diapers and wipes in partnership with the Philadelphia Diaper Bank. That spring, students also collected personal hygiene items and raised funds through a mug-painting fundraiser to purchase medical supplies, baby essentials and mobility aids for people affected by the war in Ukraine, including refugees.
