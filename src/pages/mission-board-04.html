Gathering Around the Thanksgiving Table

The 2025 Thanksgiving volunteer team

The parish’s annual Thanksgiving meal at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School has brought children, parents, teachers, and community families together since the 2018 outreach recorded in this history. In 2022, children and adults returned to serving the meal in person after the pandemic. In 2023, 135 guests were welcomed to the dinner.

In 2024, approximately 35 Sunday School children and volunteers helped serve more than 100 people. Food was catered by O’Neill’s Catering in Bensalem. The effort was led by Shyno Thomas and Neethu Varughese, with Saji Thomas, Vincy Wilson, and many parish volunteers. In 2025, volunteers again welcomed more than 100 members of the community—not only with a meal but also with fellowship and care. The annual meal was held again on November 18, 2026, at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School.

Back to School: Opening Doors through Education

Education has remained one of SGMOC’s longest-running mission commitments. Its impact has reached children locally and in India, helping students begin the school year with confidence and dignity.

Backpacks blessed for children affected by flooding in Kerala, 2018

2018. After the devastating floods in Kerala, the parish launched its September Backpack Project. The backpacks were blessed by H.H. Baselios Mar Thoma Paulose II (Bava Thirumeni). By December, 288 backpacks had been distributed in the United States and Kerala, including to children at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School, the ARDRA Foundation in Kerala, and schools in Wayanad.

2019. The Back-to-School drive supplied 20 uniforms and stationery for 50 children at Loesche Elementary School. At Philadelphia High School for Girls, 128 students received folders, and supplies were given to the school nurse’s office.

2020. The team supported learning at home through an eight-week Malayalam Vidhyalam, held July 5–August 28. It brought together 45 students and 15 teachers to learn spoken Malayalam, prayers, songs, family relationships, and cooking through videos and recipes. The project was a meaningful bridge to language and culture during a time of separation.

2021. Uniforms, school supplies, and other essentials reached Loesche Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls, Tilden Middle School, and Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. The effort included 32 backpacks for Snyder-Girotti and a $500 contribution for Tilden.

2022. In September, the Sunday School donated supplies to four Philadelphia-area schools. During July and August, optional contributions from the summer Malayalam School helped send clothing and school supplies to children in tribal communities in India, with Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai leading that project.
