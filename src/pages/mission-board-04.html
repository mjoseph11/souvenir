2023 — Reaching Higher

In 2023, the work of the Sunday School Mission Team grew into a parish-wide effort. On August 6, Joe John, Dr. Silvi Chacko and Vincy Wilson presented the SGMOC Mission Team’s plans to the Managing Committee, including two critical projects: a return to Rise Against Hunger and a Back-to-School drive. Both projects are described in the features that follow.

Earlier in the year, Sunday School children decorated lunch bags that the parish filled and distributed through Mother of Mercy House, providing 150 packed lunches to people experiencing homelessness in Philadelphia. Through a Cereal Box Challenge, the team also donated 139 cereal boxes to Warminster Food Bank and contributed clothing, diapers and toys to Mitzvah Circle during Great Lent.

Later in the year, the parish collected 109 Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, exceeding its goal of 100, and served Thanksgiving dinner to 135 people at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. In December, children and families shared carols, Scripture readings and crafts with residents of Woodbourne Nursing Home.

2024 — A Year of Expansion

By 2024, the SGMOC Mission Team was coordinating a full calendar of service, from a January blanket project to a December Christmas visit with nursing home residents. More about these projects appears in “Expanded Mission, Rooted in Bensalem.”

2025 — A Blessed Year of Service and Giving

In 2025, the Board continued the work through community partnerships and familiar projects that brought parish families together to serve. More about these projects appears in “Expanded Mission, Rooted in Bensalem.”

2026 — A Jubilee Year of Service

In 2026, the Mission Board carried its work forward through community partnerships, supporting local students and families and bringing a little joy to children facing cancer.

The parish collected canned goods for Mother of Mercy House and prepared Easter baskets for children through the Emmanuel Cancer Foundation. The Back-to-School drive supported Loesche Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls and the Bensalem Township School District. A special highlight came in October, when the Bensalem Township School District recognized SGMOC for its clothing donation to local students and families.

Plans for the remainder of the year include serving Thanksgiving dinner to the Snyder-Girotti school community, packing Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes and singing Christmas carols with nursing home residents.
