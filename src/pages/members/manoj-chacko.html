Our SGMOC Family

We have celebrated many joyful milestones together and have also faced our share of challenges. Through it all, we have come to truly appreciate the blessing of having a loving and supportive church family, especially during difficult times.

The Sign of the Cross:
A Prayer in Every Moment

By Jonahan Chacko

Have you ever wondered why people make the sign of the Cross before eating, traveling, or going to sleep? Well, the sign of the Cross is the shortest prayer and symbolizes the Crucifixion and Resurrection of Jesus Christ. It reminds us of God’s presence and protection over us. You should try to remember to make the sign of the Cross before any important events. This ensures our safety and also reminds us of God’s promise that he will always keep us safe from evil. We must also remember the less fortunate when we make the sign of the Cross and be thankful for all the blessings in our lives.

The Sign of the Cross must never be made casually and must always have a purpose.
