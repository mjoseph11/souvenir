A Journey of Faith,
Love, and Gratitude

Over the years, SGMOC has played a crucial role in our lives, nurturing our faith and guiding us along our spiritual journey. We have celebrated many joyful milestones together and have also faced our share of challenges along the way. Throughout these years, we have grown to cherish the blessing of having a loving and supportive church family, whose presence has been especially meaningful during challenging times.

We are deeply thankful for all the love, prayers, guidance, and support we have received from clergy, especially Mathai Achen, Shibu Achen, and the entire SGMOC family. Their care and spiritual guidance, together with the friendship and fellowship of our parish community, have been a source of strength and encouragement to our family.

As we happily celebrate SGMOC’s Golden Jubilee, we give thanks to God for the many blessings, treasured memories, and lasting friendships that have been such a meaningful part of our lives. May God continue to shower His blessings upon our parish, guide us in our journey, strengthen the bonds of faith and fellowship among us, and unite us as one community for generations to come.

The Sign of the Cross:
A Prayer in Every Moment

By Jonathan Chacko

Have you ever wondered why we make the Sign of the Cross before meals, when setting out on a journey, or before going to bed? The Sign of the Cross is one of the shortest and most meaningful prayers which symbolizes the Crucifixion and Resurrection of Jesus Christ. It reminds us of God’s constant presence, love, and protection in our lives.

We should make it a habit to make the Sign of the Cross not only before important events, but every significant moment in our daily lives. It serves as a reminder to place our trust in God and seek His guidance and protection as we face life’s challenges. It also reminds us of His promise to remain with us and guide us away from evil.

Whenever we make the Sign of the Cross, we should also remember those who are less fortunate, offer prayers for them, and give thanks to God for the countless blessings in our own lives. The Sign of the Cross should never be made out of habit or casually. It should always be made with reverence, faith, and a sincere understanding of its meaning.
