Message

From the Vicar

St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose

Vicar & President
St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

“Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain.” – Psalm 127:1

Dearly Beloved in Christ,

With hearts filled with gratitude to Almighty God, we joyfully celebrate fifty blessed years of St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem. This Golden Jubilee is not merely a celebration of five decades, but a testimony to God's unfailing grace, the prayers of generations, the sacrifices of our forefathers, and the enduring faith of a community that has remained steadfast in Christ.

As we look back upon this remarkable journey, we remember with profound gratitude our founding Vicar, Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor-Episcopos, whose vision, dedication, and leadership laid the spiritual foundations of this parish. We honor all the clergy, office bearers, founding members, and faithful families who labored with love and sacrifice to establish and nurture this spiritual home. Though many of those pioneers are no longer physically among us, their prayers, faith, and legacy continue to bear fruit.

This Jubilee also marks a significant moment in our parish's history as we celebrate the election of our beloved Shibu Achan to the Episcopate. His journey from serving this parish to being called to shepherd the wider Church is a source of great joy and thanksgiving. It reminds us that the true fruit of a parish is not measured merely by what it builds, but by the servants of God it nurtures and offers to His Church. May the Lord grant him wisdom, humility, and strength for this sacred ministry.
