Message from the Vicar

Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose

Vicar & President
St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

“Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain.” – Psalm 127:1 Dearly Beloved in Christ, With hearts filled with gratitude to Almighty God, we joyfully celebrate fifty blessed years of St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem. This Golden Jubilee is not merely a celebration of five decades, but a testimony to God's unfailing grace, the prayers of generations, the sacrifices of our forefathers, and the enduring faith of a community that has remained steadfast in Christ. As we look back upon this remarkable journey, we remember with profound gratitude our founding Vicar, Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos, whose vision, dedication, and leadership laid the spiritual foundations of this parish. We honor all the clergy, office bearers, founding members, and faithful families who labored with love and sacrifice to establish and nurture this spiritual home. Though many of those pioneers are no longer physically among us, their prayers, faith, and legacy continue to bear fruit. This Jubilee also marks a significant moment in our parish's history as we celebrate the election of our beloved Shibu Achan to the Episcopate. His journey from serving this parish to being called to shepherd the wider Church is a source of great joy and thanksgiving. It reminds us that the true fruit of a parish is not measured merely by what it builds, but by the servants of God it nurtures and offers to His Church. May the Lord grant him wisdom, humility, and strength for this sacred ministry. For me, this parish holds a place in my heart that words can hardly express. St. Gregorios Church is not simply the church where I serve as Vicar today; it is the church where my own spiritual and priestly journey unfolded. I had the blessing of serving this altar as a Subdeacon and Deacon, and it was within these sacred walls that I received the Holy Ordination to the priesthood. Here, I began my journey as a priest, and here, I had the joy of celebrating the Holy Baptism of our three children. Some of the most sacred and unforgettable moments of my life and family are deeply rooted in this parish. Therefore, serving this community as its Vicar during this Golden Jubilee year is not merely an assignment; it is a homecoming filled with gratitude, memories, and God's providence. I thank God for bringing my journey back to this spiritual home. As we celebrate fifty years, let us also look toward the next fifty with renewed faith and commitment. The Church is not merely a building or an institution; it is the living Body of Christ, where we encounter Him through the Holy Mysteries, grow in holiness, find healing, and learn to love one another. Our greatest achievement must not simply be what we have built, but the faith we preserve, the unity we nurture, and the Christian life we pass on to our children and future generations. Let our homes become little churches, our families become witnesses of faith, and our parish become a place where everyone experiences the welcoming love of Christ. Let us remember the past with gratitude, live the present with faith, and embrace the future with hope. May the intercession of our heavenly patron, St. Gregorios of Parumala, guide us in humility, prayer, and sacrificial love. May this parish continue to shine as a beacon of Orthodox faith and a spiritual home for generations to come. I sincerely thank our Jubilee Committee, parish leadership, families, volunteers, benefactors, and every member who has contributed to this blessed celebration. Above all, let us give glory to God, who has faithfully guided this parish through fifty years. Fifty years of God's grace behind us, a living faith among us, and a blessed future before us.

With prayers, love, and blessings,
Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose
