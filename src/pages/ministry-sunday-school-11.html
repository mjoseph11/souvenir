Our Sunday School

Some of our mission-focused initiatives include:

• Thanksgiving Outreach: Sharing a Thanksgiving meal with a school in need, reminding our students of the importance of gratitude and ensuring that others feel cared for and remembered during the holiday season.

• Mission Work and School Outreach: Supporting a school in need through service initiatives that allow our students to recognize the importance of giving back and helping other children in our broader community.

Operation Christmas Child.

Operation Christmas Child: Participating in this wonderful initiative by filling shoeboxes with gifts and essential items for children in need. Each shoebox serves as a reminder that love, generosity, and the spirit of Christmas can reach children around the world.

• Food Drives: Encouraging students and families to come together to collect food for those experiencing hardship and food insecurity.

These opportunities help our children see that being a Christian means looking beyond ourselves and recognizing the needs of others. We want our students to grow into individuals who not only know the teachings of Christ but also live them through their actions.

As we often remind our students, we are called not only to receive God's love but to share that love with the world. Our hope is to cultivate a lifelong spirit of service, generosity, and compassion in every child we teach.

Looking Ahead: Same Faith, Brighter Hearts, Bigger Purpose

As we look toward the 2026–2027 Sunday School year, we are excited to embrace our new theme:

“Same Faith, Brighter Hearts, Bigger Purpose.”

This theme reflects our desire to remain firmly rooted in the unchanging truth of our Orthodox faith while nurturing hearts filled with Christ's love and inspiring our students to live with greater purpose. We hope to encourage our children to carry their faith beyond the classroom and into their homes, schools, friendships, and communities.

Our mission remains the same: to nurture a generation of children who know Christ, love Christ, and reflect Christ in all they do. Through the dedication of our teachers, the support of our families, and the grace of God, we look forward to another year of spiritual growth, meaningful fellowship, and service to others.
