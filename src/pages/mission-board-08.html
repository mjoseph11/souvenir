2025 — A Blessed Year of Service and Giving

In 2025, the SGMOC Mission Board was officially formed, bringing together ministry representatives from across the parish. The Board continued the work through community partnerships and familiar projects that brought parish families together to serve.

Volunteers of all ages sorted and packed groceries at Arm In Arm in Hamilton, New Jersey. Our Sunday School children helped prepare more than 60 care packages for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter alongside other Philadelphia-area Sunday Schools. The parish renewed its lunches for Bensalem’s first responders and donated more than 60 boxes of diapers and wipes to the Family Service Association of Bucks County.

The Back-to-School drive provided supplies to five local schools. Parish families prepared and sent 124 Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, and volunteers served Thanksgiving dinner to more than 100 community members at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. In total, the parish contributed approximately $25,000 toward charitable efforts. More about these projects appears in “Expanded Mission, Rooted in Bensalem.”
