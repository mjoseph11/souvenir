New in 2026

Can drive for Mother of Mercy House. The parish gathered canned goods in a can drive for Mother of Mercy House.

Easter baskets for pediatric cancer patients. Working with the Emmanuel Cancer Foundation, the Mission Board prepared Easter baskets for children facing cancer, bringing a little joy to families in a hard season.

Each year the circle grows a little wider. Cans on a shelf, baskets for a child, a backpack for a classroom: all are ways of saying that no one in our community is forgotten.

A Jubilee Reflection

A jubilee asks us to pause, give thanks and look at what God has done through ordinary people with open hands. As we look back on nine years of mission, we give thanks to those whose faithfulness has carried this work.
