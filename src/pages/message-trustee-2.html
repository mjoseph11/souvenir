From the Trustee’s Desk

Slowly, the handful grew, until other parishes in the Diocese began to look to us as an example. No one would call us perfect, but we learned how to endure. We were further shaped by Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai, now bishop-elect. Achen brought us clarity, discipline, and renewed energy, teaching us to organize, plan, and build with purpose.

I did not learn this history from a book; I grew up watching it happen. My father, K. T. Ninan, was the first Trustee of this Church, and I watched him record those early offerings by hand, long before computers or budgets, long before anyone imagined what this place would become. He was not trying to write history. He was simply being faithful with what had been entrusted to him. From a child running through the basement hall to the Trustee writing these words, I have seen nearly every season of this Church’s life. Some of the hands that once carried those suitcases are no longer with us. I carry their memory, as I know many of you do.

As we celebrate this Golden Jubilee, I keep returning to four words: My Church, My Responsibility. Words like these are easy to print and easy to forget by February. But fifty years of records tell a simple story. This Church was built by people who took responsibility for it, quietly, faithfully, and at real cost to themselves.

To the Clergy who have served us, to the Trustees who kept the accounts honest and the doors open, and to the Secretaries whose records and correspondence hold us together: Thank You. Most of your work goes unseen, but it matters.

To every member who has given financially, and to everyone who has volunteered time and labor without expecting recognition: you may have assumed no one noticed. Someone did. Someone wrote it down. Your faithfulness is part of the record, and part of what carried this Church from two suitcases to the beautiful sanctuary we celebrate today.

From the pew, you see beauty. From the ledger, you see faithfulness. Fifty years of grace. Fifty years of struggle. Fifty years of forgiveness. Fifty years of generosity. And above all, fifty years of God shaping a Church that is not perfect, but has stayed faithful.

Two suitcases. Fifty years. The story continues, and the next chapter is ours to write.
My Church. My Responsibility.

Thomas Ninan
Trustee / Treasurer
