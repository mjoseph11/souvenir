SGMOC Mission Board

Feeding the Hungry and Standing with Those in Need

From local shelters to a worldwide hunger-relief effort

Youth after serving at St. Francis Inn Ministries

2018–2019. The Day of Service in March 2018 brought Sunday School children to a Philadelphia homeless shelter operated by Project HOME, where they prepared and served food. In 2019, the Fish & Loaves ministry assembled 150 care packages each quarter for children served by the Bucks County Intermediate Unit; families were invited to MANNA on Main Street in Lansdale, and youth ages 12 and older prepared and served meals at St. Francis Inn Ministries in Philadelphia.

Rise Against Hunger — November 16, 2019. Led by Neethu Varghese, the Fish & Loaves team held a meal-packing event at SGMOC from 8:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. The appeal called for 100 volunteers and the packing of 10,152 meals to make a difference in a family’s life. Parish volunteers packed all 10,152 meals, as confirmed for this history. The same ministry returned in 2023 with the enduring invitation, #ItStartsWithAMeal.

2020–2022. During the pandemic, monthly food assistance reached 15 Bensalem families through the Ivins Outreach Center in 2020, growing to 20 families in 2021; Christmas gifts were provided for 15 families. Families and volunteers continued serving neighbors when the need was greatest. In 2022, children and parents resumed in-person Thanksgiving meal service, renewing a treasured local tradition.

2023. Sunday School children decorated bags for 150 packed lunches distributed through Mother of Mercy House in Philadelphia. The Cereal Box Challenge donated 139 boxes of cereal to Warminster Food Bank, while the parish helped people in need through ongoing shelter and food support. For Rise Against Hunger, children decorated mugs and bowls to raise an additional $1,075. On September 30, 2023, parish volunteers packed 20,088 meals, exceeding their goal of 20,000.

2024. In March, 40 volunteers from SGMOC and St. Luke joined the Feed Our Community initiative and prepared and distributed 187 meal packets through Mother of Mercy House in Kensington, Philadelphia. Each included a sandwich, cookies, fruit, and bottled water. Our elementary Sunday School children also joined other Philadelphia-area Sunday Schools in preparing more than 60 care packages for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter. That effort was led by Ann Alex with Sharon Alex, Regina Thomas, Joanna Maret, and Jewel John.

2025. Another effort prepared more than 60 care packages for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter. On July 25, volunteers of all ages served at Arm In Arm in Hamilton, New Jersey, sorting and packing groceries for underserved people and families across New Jersey and Pennsylvania. That day was both an act of service and an opportunity for parish fellowship.

2026. The Mission Board organized a canned-food collection for Mother of Mercy House, continuing the parish’s response to food insecurity.
