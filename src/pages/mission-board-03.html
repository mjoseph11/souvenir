2020 — Serving Through the Pandemic

When the world closed its doors, our mission did not. The Sunday School Mission Team moved its work onto Zoom calls, phone calls and front porches, and found new ways to serve while keeping one another safe.

Early in the year, we distributed body, shoe and hand warmers through AHTN and returned in the fall with more body warmers. From July through December, we provided pizza every month for Bensalem’s first responders. We also supported 15 Bensalem families with monthly meals through the Ivins Outreach Center, an effort that continued into the fall. Parishioners also made masks for front-line workers in partnership with MMVS.

From July 5 to August 28, our eight-week Malayalam Vidhyalam brought 45 students and 15 teachers together to learn spoken Malayalam, prayers, songs and family relationships, and to follow recipes through cooking videos. Through the CARES points program, children earned points for acts of kindness, from sending get-well cards and helping neighbors with yard work to playing piano over Zoom or chalking an encouraging note on a driveway.

In the fall, we collected Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, made 30 blankets for Project Linus and held sock and winter coat drives. Children exchanged letters and cards with Woodbourne Nursing Home residents and sang Christmas carols for them virtually. We also continued sponsoring a child in India.

2021 — Continuing and Growing

As the pandemic continued, the team built on these efforts, finding new ways to connect generations and respond to the needs of local families.
