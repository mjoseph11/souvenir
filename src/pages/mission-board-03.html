2020 — Serving Through the Pandemic

When the world closed its doors, our mission did not. The Sunday School Mission Team moved its work onto Zoom calls, phone calls and front porches, and found new ways to serve while keeping one another safe.

Early in the year, we distributed body, shoe and hand warmers through AHTN and returned in the fall with more body warmers. From July through December, we provided pizza every month for Bensalem’s first responders. We also supported 15 Bensalem families with monthly meals through the Ivins Outreach Center, an effort that continued into the fall. Parishioners also made masks for front-line workers in partnership with MMVS.

From July 5 to August 28, our eight-week Malayalam Vidhyalam brought 45 students and 15 teachers together to learn spoken Malayalam, prayers, songs and family relationships, and to follow recipes through cooking videos. Through the CARES points program, children earned points for acts of kindness, from sending get-well cards and helping neighbors with yard work to playing piano over Zoom or chalking an encouraging note on a driveway.

In the fall, we collected Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, made 30 blankets for Project Linus and held sock and winter coat drives. Children exchanged letters and cards with Woodbourne Nursing Home residents and sang Christmas carols for them virtually. We also continued sponsoring a child in India.

2021 — Continuing and Growing

As the pandemic continued, the team built on these efforts, finding new ways to connect generations and respond to the needs of local families.

Through Be the Bridge, children met virtually with parish seniors, families with newborns and those facing difficult times, sharing Scripture, prayers, songs and their God-given talents. Our Sunday School children continued writing to 15 seniors at Woodbourne Nursing Home, sharing everyday experiences, favorite books and hobbies, and asking their older friends for advice.

Through Ivins Outreach, our monthly food support reached 20 families, and we shared the joy of Christmas by providing gifts to 15 families. Our spring diaper drive, led by Siji John, provided 7,521 diapers and 11,792 wipes through the Philadelphia Diaper Drive. A $250 Target gift card also helped an elderly mother purchase adult diapers.

Children shared a virtual Christmas celebration with Woodbourne residents through songs, instrumental performances and Scripture readings. During the 2021–2022 season, the team collected and delivered new and gently used clothing for Afghan refugee families in collaboration with The Wardrobe, Cradles and Crayons, and Replay.

2022 — Reconnecting and Reaching Out

As the pandemic eased, our children found new ways to bring people together through letters, meals, drives and songs. Some activities carried forward the relationships built during the pandemic, while others brought children, parents and teachers together to serve in person again.

Our students exchanged letters with seniors at Woodbourne Nursing Home from January through December. In February, they tucked snacks and handwritten notes into bags of socks for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter as part of the winter coat and sock drive.
