Our Parish History

In 1995, soon after the disappointment at Verree Road, SGMOC purchased a small church building and its surrounding property at 7721–31 Torresdale Avenue in Philadelphia. The property had already been used as a religious institution and offered something the parish had sought for nearly two decades: a dedicated place of worship. With the sale of the Verree Road property and the purchase of the Torresdale Avenue church, the dream of having a church of its own had finally become a reality after years of worshipping in borrowed and rented spaces.

The new church was dedicated for Orthodox worship by H.G. Thomas Mar Thimothios, then Catholicos-designate, later became the seventh Catholicos of the East, H.H Moran Mar Baselios Marthoma Didymos I, assisted by H.G. Mathews Mar Barnabas, Metropolitan of the American Diocese, and H.G. Dr. Thomas Mar Makarios, Metropolitan of Canada, USA and Europe. For the members who had carried the parish through years of uncertainty and transition, the dedication marked a joyful and deeply meaningful milestone in their pilgrimage.

The Torresdale years became a period of significant spiritual growth and cherished memories. Parish membership increased rapidly, and the church witnessed important occasions in its history, including the elevation of Mathai Achen as Cor-Episcopa and the celebration of the 25th anniversary of his priestly ordination. The parish also hosted numerous diocesan and Philadelphia-area programs, including OVBS, MGOCSM activities, and other spiritual and fellowship events.

Although the parish was grateful to finally have a church of its own, the Torresdale facility was small and offered limited space for a rapidly growing congregation. There was no adequate parking area, and the church lacked sufficient rooms for Sunday School, ministry activities, fellowship gatherings, and larger parish events. As membership continued to grow, these limitations became increasingly apparent.

The Torresdale church had fulfilled an important dream: it gave SGMOC its first permanent home. But the very growth made possible during those years created a new challenge. The parish now needed not merely a place to worship, but a larger spiritual home capable of serving its children, families, ministries, and future generations. Once again, the faithful began to look ahead - and the next stage of the pilgrimage would eventually lead them beyond Philadelphia to Bensalem.

A Vision Takes Root

The Journey to Bensalem – 2005–2008

With increasing membership and expanding Sunday School, ministry, and fellowship activities, the General Body recognized the need for a larger property that could serve not only the needs of the present congregation, but also those of future generations. After an extensive search and consideration of several possibilities, the parish's journey led beyond Philadelphia to Bensalem, Pennsylvania.
