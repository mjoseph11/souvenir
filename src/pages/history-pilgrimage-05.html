Our Parish History

The Torresdale years became a period of significant spiritual growth and cherished memories. Parish membership increased rapidly, and the church witnessed important occasions in its history, including the elevation of Mathai Achen as Cor Episcopos and the celebration of the 25th anniversary of his priestly ordination. The parish also hosted numerous diocesan and Philadelphia-area programs, including OVBS, MGOCSM activities, and other spiritual and fellowship events.

Although the parish was grateful to finally have a church of its own, the Torresdale facility was small and offered limited space for a rapidly growing congregation. There was no adequate parking area, and the church lacked sufficient rooms for Sunday School, ministry activities, fellowship gatherings, and larger parish events. As membership continued to grow, these limitations became increasingly apparent.

The Torresdale church had fulfilled an important dream: it gave SGMOC its first permanent home. But the very growth made possible during those years created a new challenge. The parish now needed not merely a place to worship, but a larger spiritual home capable of serving its children, families, ministries, and future generations. Once again, the faithful began to look ahead - and the next stage of the pilgrimage would eventually lead them beyond Philadelphia to Bensalem.

A Vision Takes Root

The Journey to Bensalem – 2005–2008

With increasing membership and expanding Sunday School, ministry, and fellowship activities, the General Body recognized the need for a larger property that could serve not only the needs of the present congregation, but also those of future generations. After an extensive search and consideration of several possibilities, the parish's journey led beyond Philadelphia to Bensalem, Pennsylvania.

On June 30, 2005, SGMOC purchased approximately 2.5 acres of land with a house at 4136 Hulmeville Road, Bensalem. The property offered the parish a promising opportunity to build the larger church it had envisioned. However, this land alone was not sufficient to meet the applicable zoning requirements. To make the project possible, the parish acquired the adjoining property at 4150 Hulmeville Road, consisting of approximately 1.75 additional acres, on September 18, 2006. With the combined properties, the vision of a new church campus began to take shape. The Torresdale property was subsequently sold, and the parish moved temporarily to a rented facility in Northeast Philadelphia while preparations for the new church continued.
