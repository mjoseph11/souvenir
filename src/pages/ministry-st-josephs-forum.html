About

St. Joseph's Forum is the men's fellowship ministry of St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, named in honor of St. Joseph, the earthly father of our Lord Jesus Christ. Rooted in the spiritual leadership exemplified by St. Joseph, the Forum brings together the men of SGMOC in prayer, service, and faith formation.

The ministry focuses on spiritual growth, biblical study, mentorship of young men in the faith, and communal service projects. Through regular gatherings and events, St. Joseph's Forum strengthens the bonds of Christian brotherhood and inspires men to be leaders in their homes, parish, and communities.
