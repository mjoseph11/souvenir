Our Sunday School

This theme reflects our desire to remain firmly rooted in the unchanging truth of our Orthodox faith while nurturing hearts filled with Christ's love and inspiring our students to live with greater purpose. We hope to encourage our children to carry their faith beyond the classroom and into their homes, schools, friendships, and communities.

Our mission remains the same: to nurture a generation of children who know Christ, love Christ, and reflect Christ in all they do. Through the dedication of our teachers, the support of our families, and the grace of God, we look forward to another year of spiritual growth, meaningful fellowship, and service to others.

With Gratitude and Appreciation

As we reflect on this journey, we extend our heartfelt gratitude to Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai (Shibu Achen) for his unwavering love, dedication, and commitment to our children and the Sunday School ministry. His genuine love for our students, encouragement, and willingness to invest his time and heart in their spiritual growth have been a tremendous blessing to our community. His care for our children and passion for nurturing their faith have left a lasting impression on our Sunday School family. Shibu Achen, thank you for being such a special part of our children's spiritual journey. You will always hold a special place in our hearts.

We also extend a warm and heartfelt welcome to our new Achen, Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose. We look forward to working together as we continue to nurture the faith of our children and strengthen our Sunday School ministry. We pray that God will bless his ministry among us with wisdom, strength, and abundant grace.

To our dedicated teachers, students, parents, volunteers, and parish members, thank you for your countless contributions, encouragement, commitment and prayer. Your willingness to share your time, talents, and love makes this ministry possible.

Above all, we thank God for blessing our Sunday School with wonderful children, devoted families, and a community united in faith and purpose. Every lesson taught, every activity shared, and every act of service contributes to the spiritual foundation we are building in our children.

May we continue to grow in faith, abound in hope, and reflect the love of Christ in all that we do.

May God bless our Sunday School ministry today and in the years to come!

Through every season, one mission endures: helping our children know Christ, love Christ, and reflect Christ in all they do.
