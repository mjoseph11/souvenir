Honoring our Bensalem first responders

In 2019, Hope & Love children visited the Bensalem Police Department, delivered cards, snacks, and treats, and toured the station. From July through December 2020, volunteers provided monthly pizza meals for Bensalem Police, EMS, EMTs, and other responders; these gestures continued into 2022. In April 2024, Mathew Madhathil Kurian led a lunch for Bensalem Police, Fire, and EMS in gratitude for their protection of our neighbors. The outreach was renewed in 2025, thanking responders for their “unwavering dedication, service, and commitment to safeguarding our community.”

Sharing God’s Love Around the World

Shoeboxes, international education, and a home in Kerala

Packing Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, 2024

Operation Christmas Child, a Samaritan’s Purse project, sends gift-filled shoeboxes to children living in difficult circumstances around the world. SGMOC Sunday School children have participated since 2013. The Gifts of Love ministry set a target of at least 100 shoeboxes in 2019; collections continued through the pandemic and subsequent years. In 2022, children and families donated 100 shoeboxes; in 2023, the parish sent 109, exceeding the goal of 100.

In 2024, Jessy Mathai, with Siji John and Caroline Varughese, led the project and the parish packed 124 gift-filled shoeboxes. The 2025 annual report also records 124 shoeboxes prepared and sent. The 2026 collection is underway at the time of this Jubilee history; a final total has not yet been established.

The parish’s international outreach also included sponsorship of a child in India during the pandemic, the education of children in tribal communities through the Malayalam School project, and annual sponsorship through Deepalaya. Another very personal work of charity helped furnish the newly built home of two orphaned children in Kerala. Their home, built through the generosity of well-wishers, received beds, study tables, and cabinets sponsored by SGMOC and led by MMVS. The local and international projects remind us that our circle of care extends from Bensalem to those we may never meet.

A Jubilee Reflection

A jubilee asks us to pause, give thanks, and look at what God has done through ordinary people with open hands. As we look back on nine years of mission in this history—and on the Sunday School’s earlier spirit of service—we give thanks to everyone whose faithfulness has carried this work.

To our clergy, especially our current Vicar, Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose, and Very Rev. Fr. Geevarghese Ramban (formerly Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai), whose spiritual guidance and leadership have rooted every project in prayer, and to our Associate Minister, Mrs. Ceena Mathew, we offer our gratitude and our love.
