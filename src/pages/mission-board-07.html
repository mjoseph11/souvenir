2025: The Drive Keeps Growing

The 2025 drive was conducted once again with the generous contributions of parish members. Essential supplies were collected and distributed to Bensalem High School, Henry Lawton Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls, Loesche Elementary School and Robert Shafer Middle School. The Board notes that the initiative continues to grow year after year in both participation and impact, so that students begin the school year equipped and confident.

2026: The Back-to-School Drive continued to grow this year, supporting Loesche Elementary School, Philadelphia High School, and the Bensalem School District with school supplies, clothing, and supplies for nurses’ stations, while helping empower the next generation of underserved children and strengthening our commitment to the community.
