Later in the year, the parish collected 109 Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, exceeding its goal of 100, and served Thanksgiving dinner to 135 people at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. In December, children and families shared carols, Scripture readings and crafts with residents of Woodbourne Nursing Home.

2024 — A Year of Expansion

By 2024, the SGMOC Mission Team was coordinating a full calendar of service, from a January blanket project to a December Christmas visit with nursing home residents. As the work grew, the idea for a Mission Board took shape. The SGMOC Mission Board officially formed in 2025, bringing together ministry representatives from across the parish to carry this work forward.

In January and February, parish volunteers made 27 blankets for Project Linus. In March, 40 volunteers from SGMOC and St. Luke prepared and distributed 187 meal packets through Mother of Mercy House in Philadelphia. In April, we distributed 7,820 diapers and 16,960 wipes through Family Service’s Audrey J. Tucker Resource Center and YWCA Bucks County. We also provided lunch to Bensalem Police, Fire and EMS in appreciation of their service.
