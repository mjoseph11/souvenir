Just two months later, another historic milestone followed with the Holy Consecration of the new church on May 22–23, 2009. The consecration services were led by our Diocesan Metropolitan, Late Lamented H.G. Mathews Mar Barnabas, and H.G. Zachariah Mar Nicholovos, with the participation of H.G. Dr. Mathews Mar Severios, the current Catholicos of the East and Malankara Metropolitan, and H.G. Dr. Yuhanon Mar Meletius, along with clergy, dignitaries, and faithful. The consecration prayerfully completed the parish's long journey toward establishing a permanent house of worship and formally dedicated its new spiritual home to the glory of God.

A New Chapter of Leadership

2011

The year 2011 marked an important chapter in the history of SGMOC. On May 21, the parish had the honor of hosting the Sunthroneeso (Enthronement) of H.G. Zachariah Mar Nicholovos as Metropolitan of the Northeast American Diocese. His Holiness Moran Mar Baselios Marthoma Paulos II, Catholicos of the East and Malankara Metropolitan, presided over the Rite of Enthronement, assisted by H.G. Joshua Mar Nicodemus, Metropolitan of the Diocese of Nilackal. With more than one thousand faithful in attendance, the clergy and faithful proclaimed “OXIOS” to their newly enthroned Metropolitan. Hierarchs representing sister dioceses, the Armenian Orthodox Church, and the Episcopal Church also participated in this historic occasion.
