Our Parish History

The year also marked the close of an extraordinary chapter of leadership with the retirement of our founding Vicar, Late Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos. From the establishment of the parish in 1976, Mathai Achen had shepherded the congregation through decades of growth and challenges, from worshipping in temporary locations to finally establishing a permanent home in Bensalem. Following his retirement, Mathai Achen and Kochamma returned to Kerala, where they fulfilled a long-held desire to continue serving through ministry.

On June 1, 2011, Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai was appointed Vicar of SGMOC, beginning a new chapter of pastoral leadership for the parish.

Growing in Faith, Ministry, and Service

2011–2023

Under the pastoral leadership of Shibu Achen, SGMOC continued to grow in its ministries, facilities, and service to the wider community. In 2014, the parish acquired dedicated burial plots at Rosedale Memorial Park, establishing the SGMOC Cemetery. A monument of our Patron Saint, St. Geevarghese Mar Gregorios of Parumala, was later installed there and dedicated by H.G. Zachariah Mar Nicholovos on September 23, 2018.

Another important milestone came in 2016 when the parish reacquired the property at 4150 Hulmeville Road, which had previously been part of the land acquired during the construction of the church. On August 27, 2016, the property was dedicated as the St. Gregorios Mission and Ministry Center by H.H. Baselios Marthoma Paulose II in the presence of H.G. Zachariah Mar Nicholovos, further expanding the parish's ability to support its ministries and mission.

SGMOC also strengthened its commitment to the surrounding community. On May 7, 2016, the parish held its first SGMOC 5K Run/1 Mile Walk at Neshaminy State Park in Bensalem, with 323 registered participants and proceeds supporting the church's Helping Hands Ministry.
