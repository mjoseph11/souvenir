Our Parish History

The year also marked the close of an extraordinary chapter of leadership with the retirement of our founding Vicar, Late Very Rev. K. Mathai Corepiscopa. From the establishment of the parish in 1976, Mathai Achen had shepherded the congregation through decades of growth and challenges, from worshipping in temporary locations to finally establishing a permanent home in Bensalem. Following his retirement, Mathai Achen and Kochamma returned to Kerala, where they fulfilled a long-held desire to continue serving through ministry.

On June 1, 2011, Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai was appointed Vicar of SGMOC, beginning a new chapter of pastoral leadership for the parish.
