Message

From the Metropolitan

Northeast American Diocese

His Grace Zachariah Mar Nicholovos

Metropolitan, Northeast American Diocese
Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church

Blessings to our Vicar and faithful of Saint Gregorios Orthodox Church of Bensalem, Pennsylvania!

Dearly beloved,

It is with thanksgiving to Almighty God that we offer our prayers, and best wishes upon our parish as the community celebrates her Golden Jubilee. Fifty years in the life of a parish is a remarkable milestone, bearing witness to the unwavering faithfulness of God and the steadfast dedication of generations of believers. We remember with profound gratitude the pioneering clergy and faithful whose vision, sacrifices, and commitment laid the foundation for this spiritual home. Their devotion to the Holy Church, and love for the Orthodox Christian faith, have borne abundant fruit.

A parish is more than a place of worship. It is a spiritual family, where the faithful encounter the living God through the Holy Qurbana, grow in holiness through prayer and repentance, and bear witness to His love through service to one another and the wider community. For five decades, St. Gregorios Orthodox Church has served as such a spiritual home, preserving faith while proclaiming the timeless Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ in this land.

We take this opportunity to remember with gratitude Very Reverend K. Mathai Chor Episcopos of Blessed Memory, Reverend Geevarghese Remban, the other esteemed clergy, founding members, benefactors, and all those who have labored faithfully throughout these fifty years. Their prayers, and sacrifice, are an enduring part of the spiritual foundation of this parish.

As we celebrate this occasion, we encourage you not only to look back with gratitude but also to look forward with renewed vision and commitment. May our parish strive to become that “city on a hill” (Matthew 5:14) as our Lord encourages us to become. We pray that our Lord will continue to bless your endeavors, deepen your unity in Christ, and strengthen your witness to truly fulfill the words of our Savior.

May the blessings and mercies of God Almighty be showered upon us all.

Yours in our Lord,
Metropolitan Zachariah Mar Nicholovos
November 5, 2026
