Jubilee: A Sacred Journey of God’s Faithfulness

Associate Minister

Mrs. Ceena Mathew

Associate Minister
St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church

There are moments in the life of a church when we are invited to pause—not merely to count the years, but to remember the prayers that sustained us, the hands that labored, and the generations that carried the faith forward. A Jubilee is such a moment.

In Leviticus 25, God established the Jubilee as a sacred time of restoration, freedom, and new beginnings. At its heart was this profound reminder: “The land is Mine; for you are strangers and sojourners with Me” (Leviticus 25:23). Jubilee reminds us that everything we possess is entrusted to us by God, and every blessing ultimately comes from Him. The biblical Jubilee was more than a festival. It was a declaration of God's sovereignty, a restoration of relationships, and a reminder that His people were called to live with justice, compassion, and trust in Him.

As we celebrate our parish Jubilee, we look back with grateful hearts upon a journey marked by God’s abundant grace. God has incredibly blessed our parish. Through the faith, prayers, sacrifices, and dedication of generations, He has nurtured our community in ways our founders may never have imagined. We give thanks for our clergy, families, faithful servants, children, and all who have contributed to the life of our parish. Those who planted the seeds of faith before us may no longer be physically present, but their legacy continues to bear fruit.

At the heart of our Malankara Orthodox tradition is a living faith centered on Jesus Christ and nourished through the Holy Qurbana, prayer, Scripture, and the sacramental life of the Church. We have received a precious inheritance, one that we are called not merely to preserve, but to live faithfully and pass on to future generations.

Jubilee is more than a remembrance of the past; it is an invitation to renewal. Just as the biblical Jubilee called people to restoration and a fresh beginning, may this celebration renew our commitment to Christ, strengthen our fellowship, heal divisions, and inspire us to serve one another and the wider community with love. We must also ask ourselves: What will the next generation inherit from us? Beyond buildings, programs, and accomplishments, may they inherit a vibrant faith, a spirit of prayer, a heart for service, and a deep love for the Holy Church.

As we celebrate this milestone, let us look back with gratitude, live in the present with faith, and look toward the future with hope. The God who has faithfully guided and richly blessed our parish will continue to lead us in the years ahead.

The years are our history. Faith is our inheritance. The future is our responsibility. And every blessing is a testimony to the faithfulness of God.

To God alone be the glory.
Ceena Mathew
