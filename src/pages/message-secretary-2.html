From the Secretary

(continued)

We owe a profound debt of gratitude to our founding Vicar, Very Rev. K. Mathai Korah Cor-Episcopos, whose spiritual vision and unwavering dedication laid the foundation for our parish. We are equally grateful for the devoted leadership of our former Vicar and Metropolitan-designate, Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai, whose years of pastoral care have left a lasting imprint on our community. As we enter this new chapter, we warmly embrace our current Vicar, Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose, and look forward with faith and hope to the journey ahead. Their spiritual leadership, together with the contributions of all clergy who have served our parish, remains an enduring blessing.

Through God’s abundant grace, St. Gregorios has become a model parish within our diocese and a beacon of hope in the wider community. Our greatest strength lies in our generous, hardworking parish families and our shared desire to live our faith through compassion, service, and love. We strive not only to be faithful members of our Church, but also responsible citizens and a meaningful presence in the world around us.

Serving as Parish Secretary during this historic Golden Jubilee has been one of the greatest honors and blessings of my life. To serve the very parish that helped raise me, at such a momentous occasion in its history, is a privilege I will cherish forever. This experience has deepened my appreciation for the countless individuals whose quiet sacrifices and tireless efforts sustain our parish.

As we celebrate fifty years, let us remember that our Golden Jubilee is not simply a celebration of our past, but a responsibility to our future. The greatest tribute we can offer those who came before us is to ensure that the faith, unity, and love they cultivated continue to flourish for generations to come.

To our founding families, spiritual fathers, past and present leaders, volunteers, and every parish member who has contributed to this extraordinary journey, I extend my heartfelt gratitude.

May our parish continue to be a place where faith is nurtured, families are strengthened, and generations find a spiritual home.

With gratitude, faith, and hope for the generations to come.
Mathew Madathil Kurian
Parish Secretary
St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem, Pennsylvania
