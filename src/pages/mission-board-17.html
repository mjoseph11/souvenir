SGMOC Mission Board

the leaders of the Sunday School Mission Team since 2018, including Dr. Silvi Chacko, Reny Biju, Vincy Wilson and Caroline Varughese; and the leads of MMVS, GSM, the Men’s Forum, G.R.O.W, S.T.G., Youth League and the Acolytes. Thank you for turning good intentions into good works.

Through the Mission Board, the generosity of our ministries became a shared effort of service. In 2024, the Managing Committee and Board approved 16 charity applications totaling $12,000 in donations, while MMVS, GSM, Sunday School and Youth League held 17 charity drives and events totaling approximately $15,000. In 2025, the parish contributed approximately $25,000 toward charitable efforts.

Behind these figures are blankets made for children, meals shared with hungry neighbors, school supplies packed with care, diapers provided to families, and visits that brought joy to seniors. From Project Linus and Mother of Mercy House to our Back-to-School, Thanksgiving and Christmas outreach, each project reflects the compassion of our parish and the faithful work of those who served.

These accomplishments stand as a testament to the parish’s deep-rooted commitment to living out the call to charity and compassion. The generosity of our members, both in financial contributions and active participation, has made a lasting impact on countless lives.

To our children, who have packed shoeboxes since 2013, assembled care packages, sung carols, served Thanksgiving meals and made blankets. You remind us that even small hands can make a lasting impact.
