Our Sunday School

2013–2016|Sunitha Zacharia

Faith, Fun, and Fellowship

When Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai invited me to serve as Principal of our Sunday School in August 2013, I accepted the opportunity with both gratitude and humility. Leading a ministry of more than 100 children was a tremendous responsibility, but it was also one of the greatest blessings of my life. I was privileged to work alongside devoted clergy, teachers, volunteers, parents, and students who shared a common mission—to help our children grow in their Orthodox faith and develop a personal relationship with our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.

I was fortunate to follow faithful leaders who had already established a vibrant Sunday School ministry. Under the leadership of Paul Maret, with the guidance of advisors such as Mercy Varkey and Korah Mani, a strong foundation had been laid. My hope was simply to build upon their vision while introducing new opportunities for our children and families to learn, serve, and grow together.

Ministry is never the work of one person, and I was fortunate to have wonderful people serving alongside me. Our Registrar faithfully served alongside Reny Biju as Dean. When the Registrar later accepted diocesan responsibilities, Jimmy Varghese stepped into the role. Their dedication, encouragement, and friendship helped make those years both successful and deeply rewarding.

During my tenure, our guiding theme was Faith, Fun, and Fellowship. We wanted every child and family to experience a Sunday School that not only strengthened their faith but also created lasting friendships and joyful memories within the life of the Church.

Growing in Faith

Bible projects and presentations.

Our primary goal was always to help our children deepen their understanding of the Orthodox faith. We introduced general sessions focused on Christian virtues and values that encouraged spiritual growth beyond the classroom, published regular Sunday School newsletters to keep families informed, created a Sunday School bulletin board to celebrate student achievements and communicate important events. Beyond weekly classroom instruction, students created interactive Bible projects and displays illustrating Jesus walk to the Calvary during the Holy Week. These exhibits encouraged creativity, teamwork, and reflection while making learning engaging and memorable.

Living Our Faith Through Service

Faith was strengthened through service. Our Sunday School served meals to the homeless through Broad Street Ministry, sponsored a child from St. Thomas Ashram in Attappady, organized blanket drives, began participating in Operation Christmas Child, and visited Deer Meadows Nursing Home during Christmas to share the joy of Christ through caroling.
