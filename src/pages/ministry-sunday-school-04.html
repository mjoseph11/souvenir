Our Sunday School

Reflections and Acknowledgments

In 2013, after fifteen years of service, I stepped down from my position as Sunday School Principal. Looking back on this journey as we celebrate our parish's Golden Jubilee, I feel deeply grateful for the opportunity God gave me to serve in this ministry.

We began with limited space and a small number of teachers, experienced the challenges of temporary facilities, and eventually witnessed the growth of our Sunday School in our beautiful church building in Bensalem. Through every stage, God's grace sustained us and helped us continue our mission of nurturing our children's faith.

I am sincerely thankful to Almighty God for His continued blessings, to our clergy for their spiritual guidance, to our teachers for their dedication and sacrifices, to our parents for their constant support, and to our students for their enthusiasm and commitment. It has been a blessing to witness the growth of our Sunday School and to see many of our former students grow into responsible adults and leaders in their own communities.

As we celebrate this important milestone in our parish's history, I pray that our Sunday School will continue to flourish and guide future generations in the knowledge and love of our Lord Jesus Christ.

May God bless our parish and grant us many more years of faithful service and spiritual growth. Submitted with prayers,

Paul J. Maret • Sunday School Principal (1998–2013)

Faith, Fun, and Fellowship

When Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai invited me to serve as Principal of our Sunday School in August 2013, I accepted the opportunity with both gratitude and humility. Leading a ministry of more than 100 children was a tremendous responsibility, but it was also one of the greatest blessings of my life. I was privileged to work alongside devoted clergy, teachers, volunteers, parents, and students who shared a common mission—to help our children grow in their Orthodox faith and develop a personal relationship with our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.

I was fortunate to follow faithful leaders who had already established a vibrant Sunday School ministry. Under the leadership of Paul Maret, with the guidance of advisors such as Mercy Varkey and Korah Mani, a strong foundation had been laid. My hope was simply to build upon their vision while introducing new opportunities for our children and families to learn, serve, and grow together.

Ministry is never the work of one person, and I was fortunate to have wonderful people serving alongside me. Our Registrar faithfully served alongside Reny Biju as Dean. When the Registrar later accepted diocesan responsibilities, Jimmy Varghese stepped into the role. Their dedication, encouragement, and friendship helped make those years both successful and deeply rewarding.
