Message

From the Vicar

(continued)

For me, this parish holds a place in my heart that words can hardly express. St. Gregorios Church is not simply the church where I serve as Vicar today; it is the church where my own spiritual and priestly journey unfolded. I had the blessing of serving this altar as a Subdeacon and Deacon, and it was within these sacred walls that I received the Holy Ordination to the priesthood. Here, I began my journey as a priest, and here, I had the joy of celebrating the Holy Baptism of our three children. Some of the most sacred and unforgettable moments of my life and family are deeply rooted in this parish. Therefore, serving this community as its Vicar during this Golden Jubilee year is not merely an assignment; it is a homecoming filled with gratitude, memories, and God's providence. I thank God for bringing my journey back to this spiritual home.

As we celebrate fifty years, let us also look toward the next fifty with renewed faith and commitment. The Church is not merely a building or an institution; it is the living Body of Christ, where we encounter Him through the Holy Mysteries, grow in holiness, find healing, and learn to love one another. Our greatest achievement must not simply be what we have built, but the faith we preserve, the unity we nurture, and the Christian life we pass on to our children and future generations. Let our homes become little churches, our families become witnesses of faith, and our parish become a place where everyone experiences the welcoming love of Christ.

Let us remember the past with gratitude, live the present with faith, and embrace the future with hope. May the intercession of our heavenly patron, St. Gregorios of Parumala, guide us in humility, prayer, and sacrificial love. May this parish continue to shine as a beacon of Orthodox faith and a spiritual home for generations to come.

I sincerely thank our Jubilee Committee, parish leadership, families, volunteers, benefactors, and every member who has contributed to this blessed celebration. Above all, let us give glory to God, who has faithfully guided this parish through fifty years.

Fifty years of God's grace behind us, a living faith among us, and a blessed future before us.

With prayers, love, and blessings,
Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose
Vicar & President, St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem
