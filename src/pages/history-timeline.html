Our Parish History

Fifty Years at a Glance

From the first service in Philadelphia to the Golden Jubilee, 1962–2026

1962July
First recorded Malayalam liturgical service in Philadelphia, at Late Dr. T. V. Philip’s apartment, 4900 Pine Street; celebrant Rev. Fr. K. M. Simon
Aug.
Monthly services begin at the chapel of Asbury Methodist Church, Chestnut Street
1976April
Fr. K. Mathai leads the Holy Week services of the ecumenical congregation
Sept.
Mathai Achen and his family move to Philadelphia
Oct. 10
General meeting founds St. Thomas Orthodox Church of India at Philadelphia, with Mathai Achen as Vicar
1979
The American Diocese is established
1980Jan.
The parish is incorporated in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
1984Nov. 1
Kalpana No. 69/84 of H.G. Dr. Thomas Mar Makarios establishes St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, with Rev. Fr. K. Mathai as Vicar
1987Feb.
The Holy Episcopal Synod ratifies the formation of the two parishes
1988Dec. 12
Final court decree affirms the two congregations
1994
Land bought at 9897 Verree Road; zoning stops the planned church
1995
Church bought at 7721–31 Torresdale Avenue, Philadelphia: our first permanent home
2005June 30
Land bought at 4136 Hulmeville Road, Bensalem
2006Sept. 18
Adjoining property at 4150 Hulmeville Road added
2007May 27
Groundbreaking for the new church
Aug.
First Community Awareness Day at the Bensalem property
2008Mar. 30
Foundation stone laid by H.G. Mathews Mar Barnabas
2008Dec. 25
First worship in the new church in Bensalem, on Christmas Day
2009Mar. 21
Holy Relics of St. Gregorios of Parumala received at Parumala
May 22–23
Holy Consecration of the church
2011May 21
Enthronement of H.G. Zachariah Mar Nicholovos hosted at SGMOC
June 1
Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai appointed Vicar on Mathai Achen’s retirement
2014
Burial plots acquired at Rosedale Memorial Park: the SGMOC Cemetery
2016May 7
First SGMOC 5K Run/1 Mile Walk, with 323 participants
Aug. 27
St. Gregorios Mission and Ministry Center dedicated by H.H. Baselios Marthoma Paulose II
2017Aug. 18–20
First Nuhro Retreat
2018Sept. 23
Monument of our Patron Saint dedicated at the SGMOC Cemetery
2020
Worship continues without interruption through the pandemic, by livestream
2023
Our founding Vicar, Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos, falls asleep in the Lord
2026
Golden Jubilee: fifty years of the parish
Aug. 27
Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai elected to the episcopate
Sept. 27
Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai’s final service as Vicar; Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose takes charge
Oct. 27
Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai elevated to Ramban
