Our First-Ever Sunday School Thanksgiving Dinner

Continuing our spirit of togetherness, we hosted our first-ever Sunday School Thanksgiving Dinner. The evening was filled with prayer, fun games, and joyful fellowship. It also provided a meaningful opportunity to pause and reflect on the many blessings in our lives. We were reminded of the importance of gratitude, faith, and the gift of belonging to a loving church community.

Orthodox Vacation Bible School: United in Faith

Sunday School children in faith and fellowship.

One of the significant highlights of our ministry was hosting Orthodox Vacation Bible School (OVBS) at our church for the first time in many years. We were delighted to welcome children from both our parish and St. George Orthodox Church in Fairless Hills for this special spiritual experience.

Our OVBS theme, “Be Mindful and Stay Blessed” (1 Peter 5:8), encouraged children to remain watchful, mindful of their choices, and grounded in their faith. Through prayer, Bible lessons, activities, and fellowship, the children were given opportunities to deepen their understanding of God's Word and strengthen their relationship with Christ.

Children and clergy.

Bringing our two church communities together was a tremendous blessing. The experience fostered fellowship, created new friendships, and reminded us of the beauty of growing together in our Orthodox Christian faith.

Faith in Action: Serving Others and Giving Back

An important part of our Sunday School vision is teaching our children that faith is not simply something we learn about; it is something we put into action. We believe that nurturing a relationship with Christ also means developing hearts that are compassionate, generous, and willing to serve others. Throughout the year, we have continued to emphasize the importance of mission work, outreach, and giving back to those in need.

Through our various outreach initiatives, we strive to help our students understand that even the smallest act of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone's life.
