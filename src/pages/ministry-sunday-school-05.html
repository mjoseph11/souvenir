Building Fellowship

Sonday Funday — family fellowship.

Our theme, Faith, Fun, and Fellowship, came alive through Family Nights, apple picking, pumpkin carving, Ice Cream Socials, and especially Sonday Funday. These events strengthened friendships among children, parents, teachers, and volunteers while reinforcing that the Church is one family.

Looking back, I treasure not only the programs we introduced but the relationships that were formed. I remain deeply grateful to Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai, our leadership team, teachers, volunteers, parents, and students. During this period, the Sunday School team continued to build on the well-established structure it inherited, while bringing new and innovative ideas that furthered its growth and vitality. With Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai’s vision and guidance, and the steady leadership of Reny Biju, Jessy Mathai, James Varughese, Shainey Jacob and Jinu John, the Sunday School community grew not only in achievement but in its commitment to faith, service, and fellowship.

Strengthening the Foundation of Teaching and Learning

“Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old, he will not depart from it." Proverbs 22:6

This period saw a renewed focus on strong, structured teaching, supported by well-established guidelines for both the general session and individual classroom sessions. In support of this effort, the Sunday School conducted Teacher Enrichment Workshops and Fellowships, equipping teachers with valuable tools and techniques for the classroom. Experienced secular and religious educators led practical, hands-on sessions designed to make classroom teaching more engaging and effective. Blending substance with fun, the workshops were met with great appreciation and left a lasting impact — teachers came away better prepared, and in turn, children became more engaged in their learning.

A First for Our Youth

“Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God." 1 Corinthians 10:31

The Sunday School marked a historic milestone with its first-ever Nuhro Retreat, "The Orthodox Way of Life" (based on John 4:23), from August 18–20, 2017 at Sandy Cove Ministries in Maryland — our first offsite retreat experience, drawing 90 participants including parents, teachers, and children. The weekend featured teachings from Rev. Fr. Abey George, Rev. Fr. Alexander Kurien, Rev. Dn. Bobby Varghese, Rev. Dn. Saju Varghese, and Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai, and combined worship (Evening and Morning Prayers, Confession, and Holy Liturgy) with themed workshops for separate parent, youth, and children's groups, plus outdoor activities like ziplining, swimming, fishing, and a riverside bonfire.
