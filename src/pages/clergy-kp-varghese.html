Our Clergy

Rev. Fr. K. P. Varghese

St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

Rev. Fr. K. P. Varghese

St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

It is with profound joy and gratitude that I write to congratulate St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church of Bensalem, on the joyous occasion of its Golden Jubilee. This house of worship stands as a glorious testament to God's presence and a beautiful expression of His love for the community.

While I was the Vicar of St. Thomas Indian Orthodox Church of Baltimore, I was familiar with this Church. My connection with this parish deepened when our daughter, Ceena Mathew, began her service as Associate Minister in 2023. Following my retirement as Vicar of the Baltimore church, my visits became more frequent. Upon relocating to Pennsylvania in 2024, I was blessed to become an active participant in the life of this church. The Vicar Fr. Shibu Mathai and the parish community welcomed us with open arms—a warmth that continues to sustain us till today. I am particularly thankful to the church for lovingly honoring my own Golden Jubilee of Priestly Ordination on November 1, 2025.

As the parish celebrates its own 50-year milestone in 2026, I express my heartfelt appreciation to the dedicated Vicar, Shibu Achen, for his faithful pastoral care, to the dedicated Church Managing Committee, and to all the parishioners for their love, unity and cooperation. We offer our prayers that Almighty God continues to shower abundant blessings upon this parish.

May this Golden Jubilee serve as a grateful remembrance of the grace received in the past and a renewed commitment to our shared future. I wish every family the choicest blessings of our Heavenly Father. May the intercession of our patron saint, St. Gregorios of Parumala, remain a fortress of faith for us all.

Rev. Fr. K. P. Varghese
