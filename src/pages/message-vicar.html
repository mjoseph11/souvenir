Dear beloved parishioners and friends, it is with profound gratitude and joy that I write to you as we mark fifty years of the presence of this holy parish in Bensalem. From humble beginnings, God has blessed us with growth in faith, fellowship, and service.

As your Vicar, it is my privilege to walk with you through this golden milestone. I thank all those who have served this parish faithfully across five decades — the founding members, past vicars, lay leaders, and every family who has made SGMOC their spiritual home.
