Very Rev. Geevarghese Ramban

Psalm of a Golden Pilgrimage

Very Rev. Geevarghese Ramban

(Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai) • Metropolitan-designate
Vicar of St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, 2011–2026

Fifty golden years of grace and light, SGMOC stands, a beacon bright. In Bensalem’s heart, your bells still chime, A sacred song that conquers time. For fifteen years I walked your floor, And served this Sacred Sanctuary A cherished grace I hold so dear,

We honor the past, the pioneer warriors, Who crossed the seas with faith untold. From India’s shores the flame of St. Thomas, The One, True, Catholic and Apostolic faith.

Three generations now stand tall— The founders, first, and second all— A living bridge of love and grace, Rooted deep in this holy place.

You raised a sanctuary, grand and fair, A house of incense, chant, and prayer, And carved a sacred resting ground, Where our departed peace has found.

From humble seeds, you grew to lead, A parish blessed in word and deed— Across the Northeast Diocese, you shine, A thriving branch of Christ, the Vine.

You enrich the present with hearts open wide, Where warm hospitality and love abide. In liturgy’s beauty, you anchor your soul; In charity’s mercy, you make the world whole. Through missions and outreach, your ministries bloom, And in fellowship’s warmth, there is always more room.

Now preserve the future, stand firm in His grace, And run with endurance this heavenly race. Hold fast Faith, Hope and Love Let His wisdom preside: With all diligence, add to your faith, virtue, and knowledge to virtue, self-control to knowledge, steadfastness to self‑control, And finally, Godliness to perseverance.

So the light of the Gospel will never grow dim, And the ages to come will glorify Him.

Holy, Holy, Holy, the One True God forever and ever, Amen.
May God bless St. Gregorios, Bensalem, today, and crown your future with grace all the way.

Fr. Geevarghese Dayaroyuso

St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church 4 Golden Jubilee 1976 – 2026
