“…proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubilee unto you.” — Leviticus 25:10

A Jubilee Welcome

In Scripture, a jubilee is a season of rejoicing and restoration. It is a time when God’s people remember that everything they hold is a gift, open their hands, and share with their neighbors (Leviticus 25). As we celebrate this Jubilee, we look back on nine years of mission work at St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church (SGMOC) in Bensalem, and we see that same spirit at work.

Our Mission Board: Purpose and Model

The work began with the Sunday School Mission Team and grew into a parish-wide Mission Team in 2023. The idea for a Mission Board took shape in 2024 as the parish looked for a way to coordinate charitable work across its ministries. In 2025, the SGMOC Mission Board was officially formed, bringing together representatives from across the parish.

The Board is a group of parish volunteers who coordinate the mission activities of our church. Its purpose is simple: to bring the charitable work of our ministries together in one prayerful effort so that our gifts reach people in need. Through this collaboration, the parish supports medical missions and other works of charity.

Under the spiritual guidance of our clergy, Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai and our current Vicar, Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose, ministry representatives plan, coordinate and lead mission activities. Each project has its own lead and team. The Managing Committee and the Board review and approve charity applications, and every member of the parish is invited to serve as a volunteer.

Year by Year: 2018 to 2026

2018 — Planting the Seeds

The story begins with the Sunday School Mission Team and a simple conviction: that children learn faith best by serving. In March, our Sunday School held a Day of Service, preparing and serving food at a Philadelphia homeless shelter run by Project Home. That same month, children and parents wrote cards of encouragement and prayer for the students, staff and families of Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School in Florida, and sent them in a Box of Hope and Support.

In September we launched the Backpack Project for deserving children in Kerala who were affected by the floods and they were blessed by H.H. BASELIOS MAR THOMA PAULOSE II (BAVA THIRUMENI). In November we prepared and served a Thanksgiving meal to students and parents at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. By December, 288 backpacks had been distributed in the USA and Kerala, reaching Snyder-Girotti Elementary, the ARDRA Foundation in Kerala and schools in Wayanad. A winter jacket drive supported AHTN, Bucks County and Snyder-Girotti Elementary, with thermals going to AHTN, and the season closed with Christmas caroling and craft-making with the residents of Woodbourne senior home.
