Message

From the Trustee’s Desk

St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

Mr. Thomas Ninan

Trustee / Treasurer
St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

A church looks one way from the pew and another way from the ledger.

From the pew, you see the altar, the candles, and the faces you have known for years. From the ledger, you see the columns. Twenty dollars, fifty, a little more at Christmas, a little less in January, which is how it always goes. And then there is the name that appears every month for eleven years until, one spring, the donation stops.

Behind every one of those names was a person who gave what they could, often more than they could. Some moved away. Many have gone home to the Lord. The ledger has not forgotten them, and neither should we.

To read the ledger properly, you have to remember where it begins.

This Church began in 1976 with no building, no bank account, and no committees. Everything it owned fit into two suitcases, carried in and out every Sunday by our founder, Very Rev. Fr. K. Mathai Corepiscopa, and by a handful of families who came early to set up and stayed behind to pack it all away again. Two suitcases, a few families, and a faith far larger than anything they owned.

Those early years were hard. Money was often short, and there were disagreements serious enough to have ended the Church. Instead, those families prayed, forgave one another, and stayed together. Everything that followed was built on that choice.
