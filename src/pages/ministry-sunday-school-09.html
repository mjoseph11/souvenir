• Faith in Christ to Faith OF Christ

• Hope in Christ to Hope OF Christ

• Love in Christ to Love OF Christ

This vision reminded us that our mission extends beyond teaching our children about Christ. We strive to help them develop a personal and lasting relationship with Him, embodying His faith, hope, and love in their everyday lives.

Our Sunday School ministry is blessed with approximately 100 students and 30 dedicated teachers who faithfully support this mission. Together, we are committed to creating an environment where children can grow spiritually, develop a deeper understanding of our Orthodox faith, and build a strong foundation for a lifelong walk with Christ.

Activities and Fellowship: Building Memories Together

Candle-Making Class: Sharing God-Given Talents

Candle-making session.

One of our first activities was a candle-making class graciously led by our very own Elsa Abraham and her family. Our students enjoyed learning a new skill through this creative and engaging experience. What a beautiful example of sharing a God-given talent with others! We are grateful to Elsa and her family for generously giving their time and talents to make this activity memorable for our students.

Apple Picking: Harvesting Joy and Fellowship

We continued the year with a wonderful apple-picking outing that brought together our Sunday School students and their families. The event was well attended, and everyone enjoyed picking apples, sharing laughter, and savoring delicious apple cider. It was a wonderful opportunity for our families to connect outside the classroom and create lasting memories together.
