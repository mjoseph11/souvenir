SGMOC Mission Board

Our students exchanged letters with seniors at Woodbourne Nursing Home from January through December. In February, they tucked snacks and handwritten notes into bags of socks for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter as part of the winter coat and sock drive. In April, they collected children’s and adult diapers and wipes in partnership with the Philadelphia Diaper Bank. That spring, students also collected personal hygiene items and raised funds through a mug-painting fundraiser to purchase medical supplies, baby essentials and mobility aids for people affected by the war in Ukraine, including refugees.

In July and August, optional contributions from the summer Malayalam School helped send clothing and school supplies to children in tribal communities in India, with our Vicar, Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai, leading the project. In September, the Sunday School donated school supplies to four Philadelphia-area schools. We also continued providing monthly pizza lunches for Bensalem Police and emergency personnel.

In November, our children, parents and teachers returned in person to serve a Thanksgiving meal to families in need. In December, students and families donated 100 Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, and our students shared songs and skits with Woodbourne residents in a virtual Christmas celebration.

“Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.”

— Matthew 25:40

A handwritten letter may seem small. To a senior who had gone months without a visitor, it was a visit.

2023 — Reaching Higher

In 2023, the work of the Sunday School Mission Team grew into a parish-wide effort. On August 6, Joe John, Dr. Silvi Chacko and Vincy Wilson presented the SGMOC Mission Team’s plans to the Managing Committee, including2 critical projects: a return to Rise Against Hunger and a Back-to-School drive.

Earlier in the year, Sunday School children decorated lunch bags that the parish filled and distributed through Mother of Mercy House, providing 150 packed lunches to people experiencing homelessness in Philadelphia. The team also donated 139 cereal boxes to Warminster Food Bank and contributed clothing, diapers and toys to Mitzvah Circle during Great Lent.
