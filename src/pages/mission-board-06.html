SGMOC Mission Board

Winter Coat Drive, 2020

2020. The parish distributed body, hand, and shoe warmers through AHTN and held sock and coat drives. In partnership with MMVS, parishioners made masks for frontline workers, and Sunday School volunteers crafted 30 Project Linus blankets.

2021. Led by Siji John, the spring diaper drive provided 7,521 diapers and 11,792 wipes through the Philadelphia Diaper Drive. A $250 Target gift card also helped an elderly mother purchase adult diapers. During 2021–2022, clothing was collected for Afghan refugee families in partnership with The Wardrobe, Cradles and Crayons, and Replay.

Ukraine relief drive, 2022

2022. In February, children packed socks, snacks, and handwritten notes for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter, alongside a winter-coat drive. In April, they collected adult and children’s diapers and wipes through the Philadelphia Diaper Bank. The team also collected personal hygiene items and raised money by painting mugs to provide medical supplies, baby essentials, and mobility aids to people affected by the war in Ukraine, including refugees.

2023. During Great Lent, parish families contributed clothing, diapers, and toys to Mitzvah Circle, continuing practical help for children and families facing hardship.

Diapers and wipes gathered for YWCA Bucks County

2024. In January and February, Caroline Varughese, with Vincy Wilson, Shainey Jacob, Sharon Alex, and Anna Philip, led parish volunteers in making 27 blankets for Project Linus. In April, Siji Chacko, with Jeni Chacko, Caroline Varughese, and Vincy Wilson, coordinated a donation of 7,820 diapers and 16,960 wipes for adults and children through Family Service’s Audrey J. Tucker Resource Center and YWCA Bucks County.

In December 2024, the Bibs-n-Bottles Drive, organized with the Bucks County Family Service Association and led by Shyno Thomas and Vincy Wilson, collected 4,032 wipes, 822 diapers, 10 diaper creams, 40 pacifiers, 50 bottles, and 42 blankets for children younger than five.

Also in December, the Health-n-Hygiene Drive with Robert K. Shafer Middle School, led by Caroline Varughese with Vincy Wilson, provided personal-care supplies for children and families at Shafer and other Bensalem schools. The original report recorded 76 bars of soap, 36 shampoos, 24 men’s deodorants, 24 women’s deodorants, 23 diapers/pull-ups, 100 adult toothbrushes, 100 children’s toothbrushes, 30 toothpastes, and 21 feminine products.

Health-n-Hygiene gifts, December 2024

2025. More than 60 boxes of diapers and wipes were gathered and donated to the Family Service Association of Bucks County, relieving pressure on families and promoting the dignity of those served.
