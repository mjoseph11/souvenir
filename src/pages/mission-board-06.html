SGMOC Mission Board

2023. During Great Lent, parish families contributed clothing, diapers, and toys to Mitzvah Circle, continuing practical help for children and families facing hardship.

2024. In January and February, Caroline Varughese, with Vincy Wilson, Shainey Jacob, Sharon Alex, and Anna Philip, led parish volunteers in making 27 blankets for Project Linus. In April, Siji Chacko, with Jeni Chacko, Caroline Varughese, and Vincy Wilson, coordinated a donation of 7,820 diapers and 16,960 wipes for adults and children through Family Service’s Audrey J. Tucker Resource Center and YWCA Bucks County.

In December 2024, the Bibs-n-Bottles Drive, organized with the Bucks County Family Service Association and led by Shyno Thomas and Vincy Wilson, collected 4,032 wipes, 822 diapers, 10 diaper creams, 40 pacifiers, 50 bottles, and 42 blankets for children younger than five.

Also in December, the Health-n-Hygiene Drive with Robert K. Shafer Middle School, led by Caroline Varughese with Vincy Wilson, provided personal-care supplies for children and families at Shafer and other Bensalem schools. The original report recorded 76 bars of soap, 36 shampoos, 24 men’s deodorants, 24 women’s deodorants, 23 diapers/pull-ups, 100 adult toothbrushes, 100 children’s toothbrushes, 30 toothpastes, and 21 feminine products.

2025. More than 60 boxes of diapers and wipes were gathered and donated to the Family Service Association of Bucks County, relieving pressure on families and promoting the dignity of those served.

2026. Through the Emmanuel Cancer Foundation, the Mission Board prepared Easter baskets for children facing cancer, offering comfort and joy to young patients and their families.

Friendship, Gratitude, and the Formation of Young Hearts

Companionship across generations

2018. Sunday School children shared Christmas carols, crafts, and time with residents of Woodbourne senior home. In March, children and parents also sent a Box of Hope and Support with cards of encouragement and prayer to the students, staff, and families of Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School in Florida.

2019. The Sanctuary Helpers’ Art of Living in the Scripture was a seven-week summer program for grades 5–12 on reading, meditating on, and celebrating Scripture; it helped prepare children to read Old Testament lessons in church. In Health & Wellness, Dr. Bindu Methikalam spoke to parents of teens on June 23 about mental wellness, and Dr. Jesty Moses led conversations for students in grades 4–12. Helping Hands volunteers cleaned the church kitchen and Mission Center.

2020. The CARES points program encouraged children to send cards, help neighbors with yard work, perform music over Zoom, and write encouraging messages in sidewalk chalk. Children wrote to Woodbourne residents and shared Christmas carols virtually, maintaining human connections even while visits were not possible.

2021. Through Be the Bridge, Sunday School children met virtually with parish elders, new parents, and families facing difficult times through prayer, Scripture, songs, and talents. Children also exchanged letters with 15 Woodbourne seniors, sharing stories about books, hobbies, and life while asking for the wisdom of their older friends. Virtual Christmas celebrations included Scripture, songs, and instrumental performances.

2022–2023. Letters continued throughout 2022, together with a virtual celebration of Christmas through songs and skits. In 2023, children and families renewed Christmas carols, Scripture readings, and crafts with Woodbourne residents.

2024. On December 15, children, parents, and teachers visited Woodbourne Nursing Home, singing carols, making crafts, and speaking with residents. Shyno Thomas led the Christmas outreach, keeping companionship at the heart of service.
