This leadership has not come at the expense of balance, but through it. The women of our parish carry their faith into every part of life. They manage demanding responsibilities outside the home, yet remain steady pillars at church — present in prayer, generous with their time, and faithful in service. Many have skillfully balanced busy careers with active roles at church, prayerfully bringing sound work ethics into their ministry, while carrying the lessons of their faith back into their daily lives.

A Legacy That Continues

Over the last 50 years, the women of SGMOC have been a constant, steady presence in the life of the parish — carrying it forward in prayer, service, sacrifice, and leadership. What began in 1976 as informal home prayer gatherings grew into a formally established ministry, built on faithful prayer routines, quiet service, selfless charity and mission activities and generous sacrifice for the church's growth, even as many balance demanding careers and home. Today, the ministry remains centered on prayer and worship, supporting every major parish and community event, and continues to be recognized as a model unit within the Diocese — a legacy carried forward with each new generation.
