SGMOC Mission Board

A Jubilee Reflection

A jubilee asks us to pause, give thanks and look at what God has done through ordinary people with open hands. As we look back on nine years of mission, we give thanks to those whose faithfulness has carried this work.

To our clergy, especially our Vicar, Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose, Very Rev. Geevarghese Ramban (Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai), whose spiritual guidance and leadership have rooted every project in prayer, and to our Associate Minister, Mrs. Ceena Mathew, we offer our gratitude and our love.

To our ministry leaders, the Mission Board’s Ministry Representatives and the many project leads and teams who planned, phoned, packed and drove: Joe John, Mathew M. Kurian, Dr. Jesty Moses, Alex Thankachan and Thomas Joseph who serve as Managing Committee and Board representatives and authored the annual reports with Vincy Wilson; the leaders of the Sunday School Mission Team since 2018, including Dr. Silvi Chacko, Reny Biju, Vincy Wilson and Caroline Varughese; and the leads of MMVS, GSM, the Men’s Forum, G.R.O.W, S.T.G., Youth League and the Acolytes. Thank you for turning good intentions into good works.

Through the Mission Board, the generosity of our ministries became a shared effort of service. In 2024, the Managing Committee and Board approved 16 charity applications totaling $12,000 in donations, while MMVS, GSM, Sunday School and Youth League held 17 charity drives and events totaling approximately $15,000. In 2025, the parish contributed approximately $25,000 toward charitable efforts.

To our children, who have packed shoeboxes since 2013, assembled care packages, sung carols, served Thanksgiving meals and made blankets. You remind us that even small hands can make a lasting impact.

To our families, who gave generously, brought their children to serve, and delivered supplies to schools. The heart of this mission is the family.

And to our volunteers, all members of SGMOC, whose time, effort and faithful stewardship have been the driving force behind the Mission Board: thank you. Your work has touched hundreds of lives locally and around the world, and thousands through our partners.

A Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, You multiplied the loaves and fed the multitude. Receive the small gifts we have offered in Your name, bless the hands that gave them, and keep us ever faithful to proclaim, by our deeds, a Jubilee of mercy for all. Amen.

Prepared from the Sunday School Mission Team presentation (2018 to 2023), the 2024 and 2025 SGMOC Mission Board Annual Reports, and Mission team updates for 2022 and 2026.
