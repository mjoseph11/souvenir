SGMOC Mission Board

2024 effort was led by Ann Alex with Sharon Alex, Regina Thomas, Joanna Maret and Jewel John. As the 2025 report puts it, our children showed “that even small hands can make a lasting impact.”

Arm In Arm. On July 25, 2025, volunteers of all ages from SGMOC served at the Arm In Arm facility in Hamilton, New Jersey, sorting and packing groceries. Arm In Arm supports underserved individuals and families across New Jersey and Pennsylvania. The day was marked by both service and fellowship, as volunteers met a critical need and strengthened bonds within the parish.

Christmas caroling. On Sunday, December 15, 2024, children, parents and teachers visited Woodbourne Nursing Home, singing carols, making crafts and talking with the residents during the joyous season of the Nativity of our Lord. The visit was led by Shyno Thomas.

Sharing God’s Love Around the World

Operation Christmas Child. A Samaritan’s Purse project that sends millions of gift-filled shoeboxes to children in difficult situations around the world, Operation Christmas Child has had our Sunday School children as faithful participants since 2013. In 2024, led by Jessy Mathai with Siji John and Caroline Varughese, we packed 124 boxes, and the 2025 report likewise records 124 gift-filled shoeboxes prepared and sent.

Furnishing a home in Kerala. Our parish helped furnish the newly built home of two young orphaned children in Kerala, built by the generosity of many well-wishers. The furnishings sponsored by SGMOC, led by MMVS, included beds, study tables and cabinets.
