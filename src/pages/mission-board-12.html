Expanded Mission, Rooted in Bensalem

From 2024 through 2026, our parish widened its circle of service, partnering with schools, first responders, family service agencies, shelters and food banks across Bucks County and beyond. The idea for a Mission Board took shape in 2024, and the SGMOC Mission Board officially formed in 2025 to carry this work forward.

Serving Our Bensalem Neighbors

Gratitude for first responders. In April 2024, led by Mathew Kurian, the Mission Team provided lunch to Bensalem Police, Fire and EMS as a token of appreciation for their service. The Mission Board renewed the gesture in 2025 as part of our ongoing community outreach, in gratitude for their “unwavering dedication, service, and commitment to safeguarding our community.”

Thanksgiving dinner. Each November, by God’s grace, we host our annual Thanksgiving dinner at Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. In 2024, 35 Sunday School children and volunteers helped us serve more than 100 people, with food catered by O’Neill’s Catering in Bensalem. The effort was led by Shyno Thomas and Neethu Varughese with Saji Thomas, Vincy Wilson and many SGMOC volunteers. In 2025 more than 100 members of the community were again served “not only a meal but also fellowship and care.”
