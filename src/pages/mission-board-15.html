Sharing God’s Love Around the World

Operation Christmas Child. A Samaritan’s Purse project that sends millions of gift-filled shoeboxes to children in difficult situations around the world, Operation Christmas Child has had our Sunday School children as faithful participants since 2013. In 2024, led by Jessy Mathai with Siji John and Caroline Varughese, we packed 124 boxes, and the 2025 report likewise records 124 gift-filled shoeboxes prepared and sent.

Furnishing a home in Kerala. Our parish helped furnish the newly built home of two young orphaned children in Kerala, built by the generosity of many well-wishers. The furnishings sponsored by SGMOC, led by MMVS, included beds, study tables and cabinets.

Deepalaya. Through our Back-to-School outreach, the parish extended its commitment beyond our local community by partnering with Deepalaya, an NGO in India, to sponsor the annual education of six students. This initiative reflects our broader mission of supporting underserved children and helping the next generation access education, opportunity, and a brighter future.
