Message from the Secretary

Mr. Mathew Madathil Kurian

Parish Secretary
St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church, Bensalem

A Legacy of Faith, A Home for Generations
Celebrating 50 Years of Faith, Family, and Fellowship
“One generation shall praise Your works to another, and shall declare Your mighty acts.” — Psalm 145:4

There are places we visit, places we remember, and places that become a part of who we are. For me, St. Gregorios Malankara Orthodox Church has always been more than a place of worship. It has been my spiritual home, a foundation of my faith, and a constant presence throughout my life. As we celebrate the Golden Jubilee of our beloved parish, I reflect with immense gratitude on the blessing of being part of its remarkable journey. I was once a young child growing up within this church, surrounded by the prayers, traditions, friendships, and love of our parish family. Today, that same church is nurturing my own children. To worship alongside my parents, my wife, and my children, with three generations sharing the same faith and fellowship, is a blessing beyond words. My sincere hope is that decades from now, my children will look back on their childhood in this parish with the same affection and gratitude I carry today. May they remember the friendships, the celebrations, the lessons of faith, and the people who made this church feel like home. Above all, may they recognize that they belong to something far greater than themselves, a living legacy built upon the prayers, sacrifices, and devotion of generations before them. Our fifty-year journey is a testament to what faith, unity, and selfless service can accomplish. From the vision of our founding members to the dedication of those who serve today, each generation has helped shape the parish we proudly call home.

We owe a profound debt of gratitude to our founding Vicar, Very Rev. K. Mathai Cor-Episcopa, whose spiritual vision and unwavering dedication laid the foundation for our parish. We are equally grateful for the devoted leadership of our former Vicar and Metropolitan-elect, Very Rev. Geevarghese Remban (Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai), whose years of pastoral care have left a lasting imprint on our community. As we enter this new chapter, we warmly embrace our current Vicar, Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose, and look forward with faith and hope to the journey ahead. Their spiritual leadership, together with the contributions of all clergy who have served our parish, remains an enduring blessing. Through God’s abundant grace, St. Gregorios has become a model parish within our diocese and a beacon of hope in the wider community. Our greatest strength lies in our generous, hardworking parish families and our shared desire to live our faith through compassion, service, and love. We strive not only to be faithful members of our Church, but also responsible citizens and a meaningful presence in the world around us. Serving as Parish Secretary during this historic Golden Jubilee has been one of the greatest honors and blessings of my life. To serve the very parish that helped raise me, at such a momentous occasion in its history, is a privilege I will cherish forever. This experience has deepened my appreciation for the countless individuals whose quiet sacrifices and tireless efforts sustain our parish. As we celebrate fifty years, let us remember that our Golden Jubilee is not simply a celebration of our past, but a responsibility to our future. The greatest tribute we can offer those who came before us is to ensure that the faith, unity, and love they cultivated continue to flourish for generations to come. To our founding families, spiritual fathers, past and present leaders, volunteers, and every parish member who has contributed to this extraordinary journey, I extend my heartfelt gratitude. May our parish continue to be a place where faith is nurtured, families are strengthened, and generations find a spiritual home.

With gratitude, faith, and hope for the generations to come.
Mathew Madathil Kurian
Parish Secretary
