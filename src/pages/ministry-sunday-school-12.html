Our Sunday School

To our dedicated teachers, students, parents, volunteers, and parish members, thank you for your countless contributions, encouragement, commitment and prayer. Your willingness to share your time, talents, and love makes this ministry possible.

Above all, we thank God for blessing our Sunday School with wonderful children, devoted families, and a community united in faith and purpose. Every lesson taught, every activity shared, and every act of service contributes to the spiritual foundation we are building in our children.

May we continue to grow in faith, abound in hope, and reflect the love of Christ in all that we do.

May God bless our Sunday School ministry today and in the years to come!

Through every season, one mission endures: helping our children know Christ, love Christ, and reflect Christ in all they do.
