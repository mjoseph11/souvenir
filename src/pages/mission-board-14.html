Standing with the Homeless and Hungry

Feed Our Community. In March 2024, 40 volunteers from SGMOC and St. Luke came together to prepare and distribute 187 meal packets through Mother of Mercy House in Kensington, Philadelphia. Each packet included a sandwich, cookies, fruit and a bottle of water, extending our care to neighbors experiencing homelessness.

Bucks County Homeless Shelter. In both 2024 and 2025, our Sunday School elementary children prepared more than 60 care packages for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter, together with the other Philadelphia-area Sunday Schools. The 2024 effort was led by Ann Alex with Sharon Alex, Regina Thomas, Joanna Maret and Jewel John. As the 2025 report puts it, our children showed “that even small hands can make a lasting impact.”

Arm In Arm. On July 25, 2025, volunteers of all ages from SGMOC served at the Arm In Arm facility in Hamilton, New Jersey, sorting and packing groceries. Arm In Arm supports underserved individuals and families across New Jersey and Pennsylvania. The day was marked by both service and fellowship, as volunteers met a critical need and strengthened bonds within the parish.

Christmas caroling. On Sunday, December 15, 2024, children, parents and teachers visited Woodbourne Nursing Home, singing carols, making crafts and talking with the residents during the joyous season of the Nativity of our Lord. The visit was led by Shyno Thomas.
