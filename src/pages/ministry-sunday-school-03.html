Our Sunday School

1998–2013|Paul J. Maret

A Journey of Faith and Service

My journey as Sunday School Principal began in 1998, when our founding vicar, Very Rev. Fr. K. Mathai Cor Episcopa, entrusted me with the responsibility of leading our Sunday School. Accepting this role was not easy, as our first child, Josh, was only a few months old. During those early years at our church on Torresdale Avenue in Philadelphia, we faced several challenges, including limited space and a small number of dedicated Sunday School teachers.

Despite these challenges, we remained committed to providing our children with a strong spiritual foundation. We conducted general sessions alongside individual class lessons to ensure that our students received a well-rounded Sunday School education. I am especially grateful to our Sunday School teacher at the time, Mrs. Mercy Varkey, who took the lead in conducting the general sessions and provided invaluable support throughout this period.

Our students also began building a strong record of participation and achievement through centralized examinations and talent competitions. One of the major highlights of these early years was hosting the area-level Orthodox Vacation Bible School (OVBS) at our church. This program was successfully conducted under the leadership of Mr. Korah T. Mani, who served as the Philadelphia Area Sunday School Coordinator at the time.

A Period of Transition and Perseverance

A particularly challenging period came during the construction of our permanent church building in Bensalem. Following the sale of our Torresdale Avenue property, our Sunday School moved to a temporary rented church facility in Philadelphia.

The limited space presented even greater challenges, making it difficult to accommodate our students and conduct classes comfortably each Sunday. However, with the dedication of our teachers, the cooperation of our parents, and the enthusiasm of our students, we continued our ministry without interruption.

Although the circumstances were difficult, we remained committed to our responsibilities and did our best to ensure that our children continued to learn the Word of God. This period taught us the importance of faith, patience, teamwork, and perseverance in serving our church community.

Growth and Achievements in Bensalem

A new chapter began when we finally moved into our present church building in Bensalem. With God's grace, our parish began to grow, welcoming new families and bringing a significant increase in Sunday School enrollment.

During this period, our founding vicar, Rev. Fr. K. Mathai Cor Episcopa, stepped down from his position, and Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai was appointed as our new vicar.

Under the guidance of Shibu Achen, our Sunday School continued to grow in both participation and achievement. With more space and an increasing number of dedicated teachers, we were able to provide our students with greater opportunities to develop their spiritual knowledge and talents. Our Sunday School became increasingly active in area-level and Northeast American Diocese-level programs and competitions.

One of the proudest moments in our history came in 2011, when our Sunday School won the Overall Championship in the Northeast American Diocese Sunday School Talent Competitions. This remarkable achievement reflected the dedication, hard work, and talents of our students and teachers. It was a memorable milestone for our parish and a testament to what we could accomplish through faith, commitment, and teamwork.
