During these formative years, MMVS members also attended an MMVS conference in New York, where their team won prizes in the quiz competition — a proud moment of fellowship alongside women from other parishes. Closer to home, MMVS helped decorate and arrange the dining room for weddings and baptisms, and some members gave their time to teaching Sunday school classes, nurturing the faith of the parish's youngest generation from the ministry's earliest days.

Faith at the Center of Everyday Life

The women of the parish were, in many cases, working multiple shifts to support their families — yet through it all, prayer, worship, and church remained at the very core of their lives. At home, they built consistent prayer routines and made it their mission to raise their children in the Orthodox faith, even while living far from their homeland. This quiet, steady discipline in the household became the foundation on which the next generation's faith was built.

Building a Lasting Structure

Over time, what began as informal gatherings of faith and service matured into a formally organized ministry. MMVS developed its own leadership structure, with a Secretary, Treasurer, and other committee members guiding its work. This newly formed committee went on to establish an annual calendar of events for the ministry, including biweekly prayer meetings held at the church and monthly prayer meetings hosted in the homes of members — carrying forward the very tradition of home-based worship and fellowship on which MMVS was first built.

Sacrifice and Support for a Growing Church

As a small parish working to establish itself, SGMOC relied heavily on the generosity of its members — and the women of MMVS were at the forefront of that effort. The women contributed to the parish building fund in multiple ways over the years. Through a one-day salary donation drive, the stained glass windows for the church were purchased in full. They traveled to various cities and churches to help raise funds toward establishing a new church home in Bensalem, PA. Through MMVS subscriptions and other fundraising efforts, the ministry also funded a variety of other items for the new building including the kitchen and the most recent contribution being the altar candlesticks. Their willingness to give both their time and resources reflected a deep investment in the parish's future.

Their service was not limited to their own community. The Ministry continues to support the education of 2 girls in India. Various other charity and mission projects provide support to many families in need both in India and in the United States— a reflection of the Orthodox call to serve beyond the walls of the church.
