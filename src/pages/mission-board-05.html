2023. The SGMOC Charity Fund, parish members, and volunteers supported uniforms for 50 William Loesche Elementary School students, supplies for Philadelphia High School for Girls, hygiene products for Joseph Greenberg Elementary School, and uniforms, school supplies, and educational aids for students with special needs at Henry Lawton Elementary School. In India, two students received annual education sponsorship through Deepalaya.

2025. Parish families helped provide essentials for Bensalem High School, Henry Lawton Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls, Loesche Elementary School, and Robert K. Shafer Middle School. Volunteers collected and delivered supplies so students could return to class prepared.

2026. The Back-to-School drive reached Loesche Elementary School, Philadelphia High School for Girls, and the Bensalem Township School District with school supplies, clothing, and items for nurses’ stations. In October, the Bensalem Township School District publicly recognized SGMOC for clothing donations supporting local students and families.
