Comfort for Children, Families, and People in Crisis

Warmth, blankets, diapers, and essential supplies

2018. A winter jacket and thermal drive supported the Advocates for Homeless & Those in Need (AHTN) network, Bucks County families, and Snyder-Girotti Elementary School. In 2019, Hope & Love volunteers made 26 blankets for Project Linus, a nonprofit providing security blankets to children in need.

2020. The parish distributed body, hand, and shoe warmers through AHTN and held sock and coat drives. In partnership with MMVS, parishioners made masks for frontline workers, and Sunday School volunteers crafted 30 Project Linus blankets.

2021. Led by Siji John, the spring diaper drive provided 7,521 diapers and 11,792 wipes through the Philadelphia Diaper Drive. A $250 Target gift card also helped an elderly mother purchase adult diapers. During 2021–2022, clothing was collected for Afghan refugee families in partnership with The Wardrobe, Cradles and Crayons, and Replay.

2022. In February, children packed socks, snacks, and handwritten notes for the Bucks County Homeless Shelter, alongside a winter-coat drive. In April, they collected adult and children’s diapers and wipes through the Philadelphia Diaper Bank. The team also collected personal hygiene items and raised money by painting mugs to provide medical supplies, baby essentials, and mobility aids to people affected by the war in Ukraine, including refugees.
