Our Founding Members

The faithful who answered the first call

In the earliest years of our parish, a small circle of families gathered around V. Rev. K. Mathai Cor Episcopos to plant the first Malankara Orthodox community in greater Philadelphia. We honor the charter members who joined between 1976 and 1980—those whose faith laid the foundation on which five decades of parish life have been built.
