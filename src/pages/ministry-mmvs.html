About

The Marth Mariam Vanitha Samajam (MMVS) is the historic women's organization of the Malankara Orthodox Church, dedicated to fellowship, spiritual growth, and service. The SGMOC unit conducts regular Bible studies, prayers, and participates in diocesan conferences and retreats.

The ministry actively supports charitable projects, parish events, and community initiatives. Members participate in fellowship gatherings, fundraising activities, and diocesan leadership training.
