MMVS 40th Year Celebration – 2022

In 2022, the Northeast American Diocese MMVS celebrated its 40th anniversary, marking the milestone with several diocesan events. As part of this observance, founding MMVS members from each parish — those serving since 1982 — were formally honored.

At our parish, a special recognition ceremony was held on Sunday, August 28, 2022, honoring 19 long-standing MMVS members. Sunday school children escorted each honoree with a rose as a gesture of love and respect, and the vicar presented a shawl to acknowledge their decades of dedicated service to MMVS.

These members were also recognized at the Diocesan Celebration on October 1, 2022, held at St. Thomas Indian Orthodox Church, Unruh, where they received a custom plaque and shawl.

For the Diocese's 40th-year charity initiative, our parish raised $3,160 through raffle sales. In addition, one MMVS member personally contributed $1,500 to sponsor a student, offered as an individual anonymous donation.

MMVS in Its Current Form

Since 2011, under the guidance of Rev. Fr. Shibu Mathai, who serves as President, MMVS has continued to grow in faith and purpose. At its heart, MMVS remains a ministry centered on prayer and worship. This continues to be the main focus of the group, just as it was in its earliest days — grounding every act of service in a life of faith. Members also lend their voices to the church choir, adding to the worship life of the parish.

Today, MMVS remains united in service to the church, ready to step in wherever the need arises. The ministry continues to support all major parish activities, including Great Lent and Holy Week observances, Parish Day, the church picnic, Harvest Festival, Heritage Day, the Perunal Celebration, and Christmas festivities.

Beyond the parish, MMVS members actively participate in ecumenical programs such as the World Day of Prayer, Choir Fest, and Christmas Fest, building fellowship and connection with the broader Christian community. Our MMVS members also take part in the annual Diocese Bible Quiz, winning prizes on many occasions, as well as the Diocese Annual Conference and Talent Show, and the Diocese Retreat at HTRC. The 2026 Golden Jubilee year was especially memorable, as our Bible Quiz team brought great pride to SGMOC by winning 1st prize among 29 teams from across the Diocese.
