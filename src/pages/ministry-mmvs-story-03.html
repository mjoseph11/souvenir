Sacrifice and Support for a Growing Church

As a small parish working to establish itself, SGMOC relied heavily on the generosity of its members — and the women of MMVS were at the forefront of that effort. The women contributed to the parish building fund in multiple ways over the years. Through a one-day salary donation drive, the stained glass windows for the church were purchased in full. They traveled to various cities and churches to help raise funds toward establishing a new church home in Bensalem, PA. Through MMVS subscriptions and other fundraising efforts, the ministry also funded a variety of other items for the new building including the kitchen and the most recent contribution being the altar candlesticks. Their willingness to give both their time and resources reflected a deep investment in the parish's future.

Their service was not limited to their own community. The Ministry continues to support the education of 2 girls in India. Various other charity and mission projects provide support to many families in need both in India and in the United States— a reflection of the Orthodox call to serve beyond the walls of the church.

MMVS 40th Year Celebration – 2022

In 2022, the Northeast American Diocese MMVS celebrated its 40th anniversary, marking the milestone with several diocesan events. As part of this observance, founding MMVS members from each parish — those serving since 1982 — were formally honored.

At our parish, a special recognition ceremony was held on Sunday, August 28, 2022, honoring 19 long-standing MMVS members. Sunday school children escorted each honoree with a rose as a gesture of love and respect, and the vicar presented a shawl to acknowledge their decades of dedicated service to MMVS.

These members were also recognized at the Diocesan Celebration on October 1, 2022, held at St. Thomas Indian Orthodox Church, Unruh, where they received a custom plaque and shawl.

For the Diocese's 40th-year charity initiative, our parish raised $3,160 through raffle sales. In addition, one MMVS member personally contributed $1,500 to sponsor a student, offered as an individual anonymous donation.
