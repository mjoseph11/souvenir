Our Sunday School

A Legacy of Faith, A Future of Promise

Celebrating fifty years of nurturing faith and building generations

“Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old, he will not depart from it.” — Proverbs 22:6

From modest classrooms on Torresdale Avenue to a growing ministry in Bensalem, our Sunday School has continued its mission of forming young hearts in Orthodox faith, worship, fellowship and service. The reflections that follow are shared in the voices of the principals who helped guide this ministry through different seasons of parish life.
