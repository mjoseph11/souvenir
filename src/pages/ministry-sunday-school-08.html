Our Sunday School

Challenges, Resilience and New Opportunities

Our Sunday School journey has also been one of growth, adaptation and perseverance. Even during the unprecedented challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic, our ministry remained committed to ensuring that faith education continued. It was at this time that I led the ministry as the Principal. Through the dedication of our teachers and the support of parents, we were able to sustain Sunday School classes and maintain a connection with our students during a time when gathering in person was not possible. This experience reminded us that faith formation is not confined to the walls of a classroom or church; it is a lifelong journey that continues within our homes and communities.

Investing in the Next Generation

Recognizing the changing needs of today's learners, our Sunday School ministry continues to embrace innovative teaching methodologies and curriculum development while remaining firmly grounded in the traditions, liturgy and teachings of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church.

During my leadership in the ministry, we introduced Bloom’s Taxonomy as an educational framework that encouraged a more comprehensive approach to learning, moving beyond memorization and helping students understand, apply, analyze, evaluate and creatively engage with biblical teachings and Orthodox Christian traditions. Additionally, the introduction of the Talmido curriculum has provided new opportunities to enrich faith-based learning through structured lessons, engaging activities and age-appropriate resources.

These initiatives reflect our commitment to making Sunday School meaningful, interactive and relevant to the lives of today's children, while strengthening their understanding and appreciation of our Orthodox faith.

Faith, Fellowship and Action

Beyond classroom learning, our students have actively participated in various mission-oriented initiatives and Diocese-level activities, extending their faith into action through service, fellowship and Christian witness. Their involvement in workshops, Bible quizzes, talent shows, competitions and other Diocese-sponsored programs has provided valuable opportunities to discover and showcase their God-given talents. Through their enthusiasm, dedication and accomplishments, our students have earned numerous recognitions, accolades and words of appreciation, bringing pride to our parish. These experiences have reinforced the importance of teamwork, service, spiritual growth and representing our parish with faith and integrity.

"Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it." – Proverbs 22:6

As we celebrate fifty years of God's faithfulness to our parish, may we recommit ourselves to the sacred responsibility of nurturing the faith of our children. May the seeds of faith planted in our classrooms today grow into strong roots that sustain generations to come.

With hearts filled with gratitude, we celebrate the past, embrace the present and entrust the future of our Sunday School to God's loving care.

Glory be to God for all things!

Shainey Jacob • Sunday School Principal (2020–2025)
