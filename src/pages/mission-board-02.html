SGMOC Mission Board

2019 — A Mission Takes Shape

On September 29, 2019, the Sunday School Mission Team gathered at the SGMOC Harvest Festival and shared a bold plan: six themed ministries offering “opportunities for children of all ages to volunteer time and talents for the Kingdom of God.” The team was led by Rev. Shibu V. Mathai, with Sunday School Principal Reny Biju and Mission Coordinator Dr. Silvi Chacko, together with Caroline Varughese, Neethu Varghese, Jessy Mathai, Shainey Jacob, Shyno Thomas, Sunitha Zachariah, Siji John, Sherin Kurian and Mable Philip.

Mission ministryWhat it offered
Fish & LoavesFood for the hungry, care packages and shelter meals
Hope & LoveBlankets for children, thanks to first responders, visits to seniors
Helping HandsSummer cleaning of the church and Mission Center
Gifts of LoveGift-filled shoeboxes and school supplies for Philadelphia schools
Health & WellnessSeminars on stress and mental health for parents and teens
Sanctuary HelpersScripture reading practice and care of the liturgical books

Our Sunday School children prepared 150 care packages every quarter for children served by the Bucks County Intermediate Unit, and invited volunteers to MANNA on Main Street in Lansdale. Youth ages 12 and older helped prepare and serve meals at St. Francis Inn Ministries in Philadelphia. In November the team organized a Rise Against Hunger meal packing at SGMOC, described in our feature below.

Hope & Love made 26 blankets for Project Linus and thanked the Bensalem Police Department with a card, treats, snacks and a tour of the station. Helping Hands cleaned the Mission Center and church kitchen. Gifts of Love set a goal of at least 100 Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, teaching our children to love “not in word or tongue” but in “deed and truth.”

Our children were also formed in faith. Sanctuary Helpers offered “The Art of Living in the Scripture,” a seven-week summer program for grades 5 through 12 on reading, meditating on and celebrating Scripture, which opened the opportunity to read the Old Testament lessons in church. Health & Wellness brought Dr. Bindu Methikalam to speak
