SGMOC Mission Board

The growing calendar of charitable work created an opportunity to bring these efforts under a more coordinated structure. The idea of a Mission Board took shape in 2024. In 2025, the SGMOC Mission Board was officially formed, joining representatives from different ministries and developing a shared approach to service. It did not replace the dedication of the Sunday School Mission Team; it built upon that foundation so every ministry and parish member could participate.

Serving in Our Jubilee Year — 2026

During the Golden Jubilee year, the Mission Board continued strengthening relationships with schools, families, local responders, and relief organizations. Parish volunteers supported children facing illness, responded to food insecurity, and helped students prepare for school. An especially meaningful moment came when Bensalem Township School District formally recognized the parish’s contributions. On November 18, the Thanksgiving outreach continued, while the year’s Operation Christmas Child collection remained underway. The story of mission, like the life of the parish, continues beyond any commemorative date.

The pages that follow document the mission activities themselves. Each ministry’s story is gathered in one place, with its projects, leaders, volunteers, dates, figures, and photographs, so that the work of those who served is remembered without repeating the same events in each annual summary.

Our Missions in Action

The Foundation: Six Ministries, One Spirit

At the September 29, 2019 Harvest Festival, the Sunday School Mission Team announced six themed ministries, creating “opportunities for children of all ages to volunteer time and talents for the Kingdom of God.” Under the spiritual leadership of Rev. Fr. Shibu V. Mathai, the team was guided by Sunday School Principal Reny Biju and Mission Coordinator Dr. Silvi Chacko, together with Caroline Varughese, Neethu Varghese, Jessy Mathai, Shainey Jacob, Shyno Thomas, Sunitha Zachariah, Siji John, Sherin Kurian, and Mable Philip.

In 2023, Joe John, Dr. Silvi Chacko, and Vincy Wilson brought the expanded Mission Team’s plans to the Managing Committee on August 6, highlighting Rise Against Hunger and the Back-to-School drive. As the Mission Board developed in 2024 and was officially formed in 2025, ministry representatives began coordinating projects under the spiritual guidance of the clergy. Each project had its own lead and volunteer team; charity applications were reviewed and approved by the Managing Committee and Board, and every parish member was welcomed to participate. The Board also supported medical missions and other works of charity.
