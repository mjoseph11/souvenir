The first of its kind at SGMOC, its success paved the way for many more retreats in the years that followed. Beginning in 2018, SGMOC also hosted the Unity Prayer Fellowship as part of its involvement with Building a Better Bensalem Together (B3T), bringing together members of the wider community in fellowship and prayer. When the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted normal life in 2020, SGMOC was well prepared to meet the unprecedented challenge. The parish had already established livestreaming services to enable those unable to attend church due to age, illness, or other circumstances to participate in worship from their homes. This existing capability proved invaluable during the pandemic. While many places of worship were forced to suspend or reduce their services, SGMOC continued its regular schedule of worship without interruption, reaching a record number of faithful through online participation. During this difficult period, the parish also continued to support its members and the surrounding community, demonstrating its enduring commitment to faith, fellowship, and service.

In 2023, SGMOC mourned the passing of its founding Vicar, Late Very Rev. K. Mathai Corepiscopa. From the establishment of the parish in 1976 through decades of challenge, growth, and transformation, Mathai Achen had played a defining role in the journey of SGMOC. His passing marked the loss of a beloved spiritual father whose ministry and legacy remain deeply woven into the history of the parish.
