During the public meeting, we honored many of our pioneers, senior members, past and present office bearers, and clergy whose faith, sacrifice, and leadership helped shape our parish. The celebration continued with a memorable cultural program presented by our own members and the inauguration of new initiatives, including the Memorial Wall and Vision 2030 and Beyond, looking toward the future of SGMOC.

The Jubilee year also brought an extraordinary blessing to our parish. On August 27, 2026, our Vicar, Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai, was elected to the episcopate of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church by the Malankara Syrian Christian Association. His election was a historic and joyful moment for SGMOC and a special recognition of a life devoted to the ministry of the Church. Shibu Achen is scheduled to be elevated to the monastic rank of Ramban on October 27, 2026, and, God willing, episcopally consecrated on February 4, 2027.

On September 27, 2026, Shibu Achen celebrated his final service at SGMOC as our Vicar, bringing to a close fifteen years of pastoral leadership and marking another significant transition in our history. Rev. Fr. Eby Paulose, who was ordained to the Holy Priesthood at SGMOC in 2016, will assume charge as our new Vicar on October 4, 2026, as SGMOC begins the next chapter of its journey.

As we look back across fifty years—from the first gatherings of Malayalee Christians in Philadelphia, to the establishment of the parish in 1976, through years of struggle, sacrifice, growth, and the building of our spiritual home in Bensalem—we give thanks to God for all those who walked this path before us and all those who continue it today. Our Golden Jubilee is not simply a celebration of where we have been, but a reminder that our pilgrimage of faith continues. May the generations that follow carry forward the same faith, sacrifice, and love for the Church that brought us this far.

“O Lord, You became a refuge to us in generation and generation.” — Psalm 89:1
